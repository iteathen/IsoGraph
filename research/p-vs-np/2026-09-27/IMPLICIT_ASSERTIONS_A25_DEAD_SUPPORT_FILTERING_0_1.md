## IA-347 — dead-filtered forward simulation is sound dominance evidence

### Premises

At each remaining horizon t, relation:

~~~text
R_t(p,q)
~~~

satisfies:

1. current acceptance transfer:

~~~text
R_t(p,q)
AND
CURRENT(p)
    ->
CURRENT(q);
~~~

2. DEADHAT is sound as in IA-346;

3. for every legal p label not certified dead:

~~~text
R_t(p,q)
AND
NEXT(p,a)=p'
AND
NOT DEADHAT_t(p,a)

    ->

exists q':
    NEXT(q,a)=q'
    AND
    R_(t-1)(p',q').
~~~

### Body

~~~text
R_t(p,q)
    ->
p <=F q.
~~~

### Proof

Induct on remaining horizon.

Any accepting continuation from p is either:

- empty/current, transferred by premise 1; or
- a·s through child p'.

Because a·s accepts, p' is live, so sound DEADHAT cannot certify a dead.

Premise 3 supplies q' under the same label and the induction hypothesis transfers s.

### Disposition

ADMITTED EXACT CONDITIONAL.
