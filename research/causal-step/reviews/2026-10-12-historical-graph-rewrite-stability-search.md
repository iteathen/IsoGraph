## Why recovery would matter to Causal Step

A real multi-rule, multi-seed campaign is much more informative for the researcher’s current question about causal-depth/particle-like emergence than applying one parent-selection rule to one graph. But its **steady state** must be reconstructed as an explicit observation and scope, such as:

- a frozen graph modulo a stated node relabeling equivalence;
- a periodic orbit or recurring graph-isomorphism class;
- statistical stationarity of structural invariants (e.g. degree, connectivity, motif counts) despite continuing rewrites;
- persistent recognizable localized motifs with bounded drift;
- finite-horizon survival only, which is **not** an asymptotic steady-state claim.

Future recovery protocol: preserve original generator/source hash and run files first; extract seeds, rule forms, node/hyperedge encoding, update scheduling, rewrite count, resource caps, rule/seed sampling weights, and stopping criteria. Then independently validate top candidates against longer horizons, alternate initial conditions and other rules. This avoids the previously demonstrated artificial-dimension ceilings, projected circulation artifacts and cap-created graph fragmentation.
