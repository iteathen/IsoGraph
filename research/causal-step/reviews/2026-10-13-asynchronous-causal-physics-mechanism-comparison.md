## 1. Explicit comparison boundary

The Causal Step working premises remain **exactly two**: mostly emergent reality from simple conditions, and the investigator's newly defined `causal step` terminology (not Einstein's `c`). Time means succession of causal events, not a physical clock. No dimensionality bound, coordinates, masses, energies, metric, Hilbert space, action or entropy dynamics is granted to the primitive generator. The exploratory entropy/back-pressure idea remains *distinct* from statistical, von Neumann, relative, graph-cut, holographic and spectral entropies.

Model structural content at five progressively stronger levels:

- **E0 — observer equality:** selected counts, adjacency shadows, entropies, spectra or effective cosmological curves agree.
- **E1 — typed incidence and state:** preserve genuine multi-input/multi-output relations, directed roles, multiplicity, relation identity, event occurrence and primitive/nonprimitive status, with an injective or justified quotient map.
- **E2 — evolution and composition:** preserve enabled operations, causal succession, concurrency/independence, full affected boundary, and the composition of lawful transitions under a specified scheduler class.
- **E3 — intervention/response:** preserve conditional enablement and reachable outcomes for all interventions in the **declared scope**; a probabilistic version would also require kernel preservation, not just possible outcomes.
- **E4 — derived physical observation:** preserve particular continuum/quantum/cosmological readouts within separately justified approximations and measurement protocols.

E0 or E4 without E1–E3 does **not** establish equivalence of microscopic dynamics. E1–E3 do not by themselves prove that any actual physics is represented. A pairwise shadow cannot be assumed to reconstruct k-ary joint preconditions. Unknown/higher-order residuals must remain explicit; if any layer is absent, mark the proposed map as a *comparison/forgetful functor*, not an equivalence.

## 2. Newly located cross-field mechanisms (primary-source anchored)

**N1: asynchronous causal rewriting as a control on causal succession.** Arrighi, Costes and Maignan, [*Causal graph rewriting* (2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25188), define asynchronous local rewriting of directed acyclic graphs whose directed edges are both computational dependencies and locality restrictions. The authors' abstract reports that valid rewrite sequences inherit a locality/extensivity property and that 1D CA can be simulated. Their particle and time-dilation examples are mathematical illustrations, **not** phenomenological confirmation of matter or GR. Important boundary: their directed graphs have binary edges, whereas Causal Step's candidate generator uses true k-way hypergraph incidence. A proposed correspondence must preserve joint inputs, events and interventions across a specified rewrite category. Only the abstract/source metadata of this particular 2026 preprint were accessible in the present scan; its full theorem proof was **not** independently checked.

A related [2025 *Space-time reversible graph rewriting*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03296) gives local sufficient reversibility conditions across spacelike cuts. Its **actual name algebra** includes integer time tags `t.u`, left/right subnames `u.l` and `u.r`, and a merging operation; it uses finite port-labelled DAGs. These are formal bookkeeping resources, but their necessity to the proofs must be assessed before claiming the construction introduces no time/valence restrictions. The older [Arrighi–Martiel–Perdrix reversible causal graph dynamics](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.04368v3) prove local block representation/invertibility in a **synchronous, bounded-degree** graph setting and almost vertex preservation; this is not an uncapped k-ary universe generator. Compare the three papers as distinct models, not one interchangeable label.

**N2: multi-parent event selection can be mathematically prior to geometry.** Cortês and Smolin, [*The universe as a process of unique events*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.90.084007) and [*Quantum energetic causal sets*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.90.044035), place events first and derive an effective spacetime embedding in a semiclassical regime. The former describes deterministic generation from *pairs of progenitor events*, selected using distinctions between causal pasts; experiments are 1+1-dimensional. However links carry energy/momentum with event conservation laws, and amplitudes are also introduced in the quantum extension: energy and quantum amplitudes are **inputs**, not consequences of raw ancestry. Their later work [Cohen et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.102.124028) explicitly demonstrates that *birth order*, *momentum-flow partial order* and *emergent geometry's causal order* can differ. Therefore causal-order-preservation is a **test obligation**, not an automatic inference from their vocabulary.

**N3: vertex composition can be more fundamental than distant spatial coincidence.** Amelino-Camelia, Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman and Smolin, [*Principle of relative locality*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.84.084010), construct observer-dependent spacetime descriptions from a phase-space/momentum-space account of interacting worldlines. Multi-particle conservation at a vertex, possibly nonlinear momentum composition, is a useful analogy to higher-order incidence and the near/far signal paradox. But momentum, phase space and interaction action are inputs; there is **no** derivation from a pre-energy hypergraph. An exact test must retain event-vertex arity and associativity/ordering data rather than imposing a familiar vector-sum conservation law.

**N4: order interval counts to downstream scalar curvature.** Benincasa and Dowker, [*Scalar curvature of a causal set*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.181301), derive a retarded nonlocal scalar operator from causal-order intervals and its continuum approximation `Box - R/2` in a specified **four-dimensional manifoldlike** regime. This is a concrete bridge from discrete *causal observer statistics* to geometric response. It is **not** a derivation of dimension four, a metric, gravity dynamics, or a unique underlying k-way process. The [arXiv v4 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2725) warns that a typo remained in the published version; use corrected text if calculating the operator. Before using the 4D coefficients, test dimension-free order-interval observer maps, relabel invariance and noninjectivity against different higher-order incidences.

**N5: quantum theory from composable operations versus operations assuming quantum theory.** Chiribella, D'Ariano and Perinotti [*Informational derivation of quantum theory*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.84.012311) obtain quantum theory from causality, perfect distinguishability, ideal compression, local distinguishability, pure conditioning and **purification** postulates on probabilistic operational theories; these rich axioms are **assumed**, not produced by an untyped graph. Oreshkov, Costa and Brukner [*Quantum correlations with no causal order*](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms2076) assume local quantum instruments and allow valid process matrices without definite global causal order; some violate causal inequalities. Their result supplies an **adversarial limit** for any claim that all quantum behavior comes from one fixed classical DAG. Neither establishes that the raw Causal Step generator automatically yields Hilbert structure. Distinguish formal local quantum validity, physical realization, and underlying microscopic causal order.

**N6: the Dirac continuum limit is a strong *conditional* target.** D'Ariano and Perinotti [*Derivation of the Dirac equation from principles of information processing*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.90.062106) derive a low-wavevector, low-inertial-mass 3D Dirac limit of a unitary, homogeneous, isotropic quantum cellular automaton; high-energy Lorentz covariance is distorted. **Three spatial dimensions, the lattice/graph locality, mass parameter, Hilbert state and synchronous dynamics are set upstream.** Borrow its continuum-dispersion **benchmark only after** geometry and states have independent causal constructions.

**N7: vacuum energy can fail to source curvature even when quantum matter exists.** Kaloper and Padilla's [2014 vacuum-energy sequestering](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.091304) cancels gravitational effects of large matter-sector vacuum contributions via global constraints involving spacetime four-volume; the original construction required finite spacetime/recollapse. Their [2016 local reformulation](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.051302) uses auxiliary fields, leaving a radiatively stable **undetermined** curvature contribution measured empirically. Both are mathematical ideas about *response constraints*, not evidence that cosmic acceleration is an illusory observation or that matter/metric/energy have been derived. [Unimodular gravity](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.40.1048) likewise turns Λ into an integration constant under a specified background volume form without computing its observed small value. Import these only as eventual **effective** consistency checks; neither is a primitive hypergraph birth rule.

## 5. Completed exact structural diagnostic

[Executable JavaScript](../experiments/2026-10-13-higher-order-causal-projection-and-scheduling.mjs) and [machine-readable observed result](../experiments/results/2026-10-13-higher-order-causal-projection-and-scheduling.json) were **actually run** in a local V8 execution environment and saved to this research branch. They compare:

- H_joint has **one ternary incidence** `{a,b} -> c` with a joint two-source precondition.
- H_split has **two binary incidences** `a -> c` and `b -> c`, either individually sufficient.
- Both have the identical pairwise shadow `a>c, b>c`, and hence identical order-reachability information on those nodes.
- Enumerating all four subsets of `{a,b}`: empty and both-sources agree; **only-a and only-b disagree**. Thus 2/4 interventions distinguish the states even when the pairwise graph observer cannot.
- All **six permutations of three vertices** were checked and preserved both shadow-equality and intervention responses under relabeling.
- Two disjoint rewrite rules give the same terminal active set in both admissible orders. An overlapping causal dependency `{a,b}->c` then `{b,c}->d` **cannot** be swapped when c is initially absent. Therefore universal confluence/commutation cannot be inferred from disjoint independence.

This is an **exact finite noninjectivity / rewrite-scheduling witness**, not an eight-thousand-rewrite test, a proof of the 2026 paper's theorem, a cosmological simulation, a physical measurement, or a test of quantum causality. A combined causal-graph observer and any purely order-interval operator cannot reconstruct the difference *from their common binary shadow alone*. Full incidence+intervention data retain it.
