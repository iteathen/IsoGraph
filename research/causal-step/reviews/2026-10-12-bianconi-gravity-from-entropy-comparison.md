## 1. What Bianconi actually proposes

### Continuum GfE (2025; published corrected version)

The paper **starts** from a **torsion-free, d-dimensional manifold carrying a Lorentzian metric** `g_{μν}` and its compatible connection. It describes matter via Dirac–Kähler-type differential forms, including 0-, 1- and 2-forms. The matter fields and geometric operators determine a second, **matter-induced metric/operator**. The proposed gravitational action is constructed from a generalized **quantum relative-entropy-like** comparison between the original metric and the induced metric. Variation leads to modified gravitational field equations; a specified low-coupling regime recovers an Einstein–Hilbert-like gravitational action. The auxiliary `G`-field facilitates a rewritten form with a proposed dynamical effective cosmological term.

- The **information-theoretic coupling / dynamical gravitational equations** are the derived object **conditional on those inputs**.
- The **Lorentzian metric/manifold, dimension, differential-form field types, operator framework, action and chosen variational principle are inputs**, not spontaneously born in this calculation.
- It is therefore an important potential **emergent gravitational dynamics** construction, **not** evidence that a coordinate-free causal graph intrinsically selects a metric or a fixed number of spacetime dimensions.

Source: [corrected journal publication](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.111.066001), introduction/Sec. II and gravitational action Sec. III (around paper equations 38–45). Unlike a claim of “entropy creates gravity from nothing,” the published theory explicitly starts with geometry and matter.

### Discrete precursor (2024; closer to our graphs)

The discrete theory starts from a **higher-order network, or cell complex**, equipped with node, edge and 2-cell structure. Its dynamical matter degrees of freedom are encoded by **topological spinors / cochains** on 0-, 1-, and 2-cells, and the **discrete Dirac/Hodge operators** use boundary maps and the chosen metric.

Specifically, the paper considers a positive-definite Hermitian metric matrix `𝒢` and a matter/gauge-induced operator/metric matrix `G`, and introduces (Eq. 20) an action of the form:

```text
S₊ = σ Tr(log 𝒢)
     + Tr[𝒢 (log 𝒢 − log G)]
     − Tr 𝒢
```

(with paper-specific operator conventions and a companion `S₋` in Eq. 21). The first term is proportional to log metric volume and the second compares network metric and matter-induced metric. **These are postulated action ingredients**, and stationarity/variation—not an automatically prescribed local random walk, a literal scalar force or an unrestricted “entropy growth” algorithm—is used to derive the field equations.

**Key limitation for Causal Step:** The discrete paper **presupposes** an input cell complex and metric matrix. It states that its dynamical analysis is for **fixed underlying network topology**; a later section considers an explicitly **3D lattice topology** as a specialization. Neither 2-cell topology nor 3D lattice should be rebranded as *emergent* from an unconstrained causal predecessor graph. Graph cycles by themselves are not automatically specified, consistently oriented 2-cells.

The discrete paper offers **mathematical operators and falsifiable structural interfaces** worth adapting, but such an adaptation is **a new model**, not an execution or proof of Bianconi's original equations.

## 2. Why this is directly relevant to our discarded “spatial tax”

The researcher observed that original Model A seemed to charge a cost for being separated from other nodes, effectively encouraging clustering. Source audit showed this came from **explicit spring rest lengths and Euclidean displacement**. It was not an independently emergent entropy or physical gravitational principle.

An information-theoretic action could provide a **candidate organizing mechanism** without *explicitly* declaring a central Newtonian attraction. But merely replacing spring potential with a penalty called “entropy,” “relative entropy,” “information mismatch,” or “emergence tax” would repeat the problem unless the compared variables, their distributions, the microstate ensemble, and evolution law are independently motivated.

In particular:

- **Shannon entropy** of a specified probability distribution, **quantum relative entropy** of compatible positive operators, **thermodynamic entropy**, and the researcher's broader **speculative primitive organizing/back-pressure** are **different constructs**.
- Conventional `D(ρ||σ)` requires **meaningful `ρ`, `σ` (and support/normalization choices)**. A metric tensor/operator is not automatically a normalized quantum state; Bianconi carefully defines a generalized local operator formalism and the 2024 action contains extra trace terms.
- Relative entropy is **not** automatically a distance, force, causal ancestry metric, or time-step update; even its monotonicity/sign under dynamics is model-specific. A variational action being stationary **does not imply** a dynamical algorithm minimizes that action on each causal update.
- “No imposed Newtonian mechanics” also means **no inverse-square law, global center-of-mass attraction, predetermined target length or compulsory conservation projection** is inserted simply because entropic-gravity literature exists.

### Source-faithful vs hypothesis-motivated comparison

**Bianconi-original reproduction** would require precisely her metric, Hodge/Dirac operator, fields, action, positivity assumptions, boundary conditions, and variations. That is a *separate* higher-level validation, not obtainable merely by labeling adjacency matrices as density operators. **Causal Step primitive-graph experiments** must stay source-faithful to their independently defined relation/causal semantics. The *comparison* comes only when both are translated to a declared common structural question with explicit differences.

### Resource strategy

The graph-only experiments have thousands of nodes; generic dense eigenvalue decomposition / matrix logarithms may scale cubically and exceed available memory/runtime. Begin with small frozen sample graphs and provably invariant diagnostics, then use sparse matrix tools or controlled spectral approximations only with measured error and approximation scope. This memory/computation bound is an **operational limit**, not a physical cutoff.

## 4. Relationship to Causal Step's two premises

1. **Emergent reality from simple conditions:** GfE motivates investigating whether geometry/gravity are higher-level consequences of deeper informational organization. But neither 2024 nor 2025 Bianconi theory presently supplies the *primitive generative rule* from which all geometry emerges.
2. **Causal step as a separately defined light-speed unit; time as causal succession:** GfE's relativistic action assumes a Lorentzian metric and uses ordinary continuum spacetime in its derivation. It does not independently establish the meaning of an elementary causal step, an emitter-to-detector single-link semantics, or causal-event time. These remain separate questions.
