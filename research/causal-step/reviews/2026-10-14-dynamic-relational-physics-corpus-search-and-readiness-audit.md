## 1. Why the earlier approach would produce a biased comparison

Earlier Causal Step research sampled selected causal-set, holography, topology, quantum gravity and dark-sector candidates, plus a few externally inspired structures. It had exact native T1–T4 data, but the **comparative source universe was neither exhaustively scoped nor sufficient to justify a comprehensive test**.

A visually similar network, a matching spectral histogram, an effective geometry or a shared term such as entropy can conceal different:
- typed objects, hyperedge rank and **occurrence multiplicity**;
- event enabling, inhibitor/state guards, operational inputs and **interventions**;
- actual source rule, local state mutation, resource limits and state/birth/death;
- composition/order, concurrency and higher-event coherence;
- postulated vs derived metric, physical time, quantum Hilbert state and energy;
- source observables and mathematical/physical equivalence notions.

Existing T3 and T4 explicitly show the danger: *identical full binary causal order* can hide distinct k-way source enablement, and 660 pairwise-initial-commuting native three-event cases can block at the third operation. A complete **bibliography** cannot substitute for a complete **mechanism-level comparison**.

The target corpus must therefore preserve *the source laws and their assumption burdens*, not only paper titles. The user’s intended foundational meaning of an organizing/back-pressure tendency called entropy remains separate from standard entropy notions and is **not** preinstalled in any primitive source rule.

## 2. Operational definition of reasonable completeness

A literally exhaustive survey of *all possible or published physics* has no well-specified finite universe. Completeness is meaningful only relative to an **explicit source/search universe, representation inclusion rules, discovery procedure and stopping conditions**.

The search target is **every documented class relevant to physics** where a candidate structure can be described as typed objects plus relational **evolution, composition, enabling or observation**, even when the authors do **not** call the work “relational physics.” Include adjacent formal mathematics and well-tested effective phenomenology as distinct evidence classes. Do not impose physical time, spacetime dimension, Hilbert states, energy or probabilistic laws into the Causal Step substrate merely because an external paper does.

Five inclusion lanes:
1. **Generative fundamentals:** causal sets, stochastic growth, energetic causets, group field theory, loop/spinfoams, causal fermion systems, graphity, quantum gravity, emergent topology/metric, network geometry;
2. **Actual physical/collective dynamics:** hypergraphs, simplicial dynamics, emergent gauge/string nets, fractons, adaptive networks, cellular automata, statistical mechanics, chemical/biological reaction systems, field/network couplings, nonlocal effective gravity;
3. **Formal dynamic-composition mathematics:** Petri nets, κ-calculus, double-pushout rewriting, concurrency, event structures, higher categories, operads, quantum processes, Markov categories and compositional open systems;
4. **Observation/reconstruction mathematics:** coarse-graining, renormalization, causal inference, intervention sufficiency, data processing, relational reference frames, operational equivalence, information bottlenecks, inverse identifiability and holographic coding;
5. **Independent physics response constraints:** light bending, Weyl/slip, local gravity, galaxy acceleration, merging cluster lensing, CMB/cosmic shear, BAO/SN, gravitational waves, laboratory and astrophysical corrections, with source observable distinguished from interpreted invisible mass/field.

Source records with strong **preloaded physics** are *included*, explicitly labeled as **conditional comparisons**, not discarded. A mathematically negative or contradictory result has priority over a less qualified positive claim. Nonphysical graph aesthetic analogies are excluded from the structural-match tier but may remain indexed as negative controls.

For every lane, declare a search strategy by at least two independent families of terminology. E.g. “causal geometry” and “compositional rewriting”; “quantum information emergence” and “reaction/Markov observational sufficiency.” This catches work shaped like Causal Step that uses completely different vocabulary.

## 4. Source search and adjudication protocol

Adopt a **PRISMA-inspired transparent research-search reporting discipline**, not a false claim of a completed formal PRISMA systematic review. [PRISMA 2020](https://www.bmj.com/content/372/bmj.n71) supplies transferable principles: explicitly record inclusion/exclusion, information source, exact query and date, duplicate identification, potentially relevant excluded papers, evidence/quality appraisal and publicly available extracted data. It was designed primarily for health intervention systematic reviews; this theoretical physics source survey is **not** a meta-analysis of randomized trials.

### Search records, to be completed

A recorded search attempt must contain **date, database/engine or publication source, exact full query/filters, retrieved count, screened identifiers, kept/dropped decisions, duplicate map, and newly discovered *formal structural family***. Current v0.3 entries come from earlier Causal Step registrations plus this turn's field-focused web/publisher/arXiv screening. These screens were **not exported as a complete machine-replayable set of every query and every rejected search hit**, nor was a licensed citation-index API exhaustively queried. It would be false to mark that gate complete.

Recommended independent queries per lane (future template, not assertions already run in every database):
- causal/set/rewrite: "causal set" + dynamics + manifoldlikeness; graph rewriting + concurrency + critical pairs; "reaction systems" + inhibitors; "hypergraph" + causal enabling + composition;
- quantum/information: "process tensor" + multi-time interventions; quantum reference frames + causal order; "operational probabilistic" + emergence; "relative entropy" + sufficient statistic; "holographic" + erasure reconstruction;
- networks/physics: higher-order synchronization; adaptive graph state-dependent interactions; quantum link/gauge; string-net collective modes; stochastic nonequilibrium graph cycles; nonlocal memory/influence;
- geometry/gravity: causal geometry + conformal light rays; simplicial/spinfoam actions; topology under incidence versus simplicial closure; galaxy lensing + matter dynamics + alternative gravity; corpus critical counterexamples.

Search archives include DOI publisher pages/APS, IOP, SIAM, Springer, Wiley, Nature, Cambridge, peer-reviewed physics/journal portals and arXiv abstracts; for rule chemistry/biophysics, PubMed-indexed sources. High-priority domains next require **in-paper cited and citing bibliography sweeps**, not merely searching more synonyms. Label inaccessible original technical text **unverified**, not assumed to agree with an abstract.

### Inclusion, exclusion and bias controls

- **Include:** a source defines an explicitly relational process or supplies a theorem about what it preserves/loses; or supplies a real observational constraint on proposed physical output. Effective theories with spacetime already assumed remain available, but annotated *effective comparison*.
- **Keep separate:** same source may contain multiple non-equivalent formulations. Distinguish equation variants, source corrections and external source assumptions. Keep known negative evidence and critiques.
- **Exclude as direct process equivalence:** a static co-incidence graphic, philosophical metaphor, impressive numerical curve with no defined transition, or an abstract model that matches after importing new Hilbert/metric/energy primitives. The bibliographic item can be retained with a reason.
- **Reject unsupported physical inference:** observationally fitted apparent dark-matter force laws or QRE expressions cannot independently prove a fundamental causal substrate; dark-particle models are not automatically excluded.
- **Avoid selection on outcome:** do not decide an approach is relevant because its predictions resemble the eventual target; prioritize operators and *internal source semantics* before looking at favorable physical response curves.

Source quality is independently graded by whether an **original paper actually specifies operations**, whether those equations and assumptions are inspected, mathematical proof/scope checked, executable finite/null cases reproduced, a representation map proved/tested, and physics validated empirically.

For candidate geometry, prove actual manifold/cell/metric assumptions rather than creating simplicial closure to make topology available. ## 6. Operational completeness and test-readiness gates

**Coverage gate C0 — taxonomy stability:** Check all 16 top-level domains and cross-field sources; record candidate *new* domains and classes, not forbid them. All 16 bins currently nonempty, but that is only minimal breadth.

**Search gate C1 — source saturation:** For **each** domain, run at least two **independently formulated search routes** plus backward and forward citations from landmark and dissenting papers, then a new held-out scan. Operationally call the family inventory "source-saturated for this frozen scope" only after two successive completed rounds introduce **no new operation-defined family** with the same declared inclusion criteria. This is a reproducible stopping rule, **not a theorem that no other scientific work exists**. The third tranche found **eleven additional relevant sources** in previously sparse domains, which already proves the current searches have **not** saturated.

**Depth gate C2 — exact extraction:** Priority-A representative papers must have the original **operational equations/definitions and cited locations** checked, not only abstracts. Every candidate connection must specify the map to native high-order dynamics or the missing pieces that prevent it. The **27** currently missing fields must be explicitly adjudicated; observational sources may be marked **not applicable** for dynamics rather than filled by speculation.

**Contradiction gate C3 — negative completeness:** Include dissenting/source-level counterexamples and papers falsifying over-strong equivalences (e.g. hypergraph/stabilizer entropy cone gap, dynamic higher-order-versus-simplicial differences, causal-set non-manifoldlike obstructions, entropic gravity empirical tensions, T3/T4 noninjectivity). Preserve negative comparisons in the final library.

**Reproduction gate C4 — actual comparison data:** For any model taken to a native cross-theory test, capture source algorithm or exact formal transition equation, permitted initial states, source assumptions, equivalence criterion and a **small reproduced positive/negative control**; only then compare mechanism-level effects. Authors' external computations are evidence, not independently reproduced results in this repository by merely citing a DOI.

**Physics gate C5 — independent outcomes:** Even when a mathematical process map succeeds, physical gravity/quantum/particle claims require a further observational output map and independent empirical constraints. O01–O14 cosmology and lensing gates remain separate; passing C0–C4 does not imply C5.

**Current disposition:** All categories have initial source records, but **C1–C4 are not passed** and no native physics omnibus test is authorized by corpus count alone. This correction postpones the proposed broad native signal/geometry test until the pretesting comparison data are ready; it does **not prohibit** a separately justified formal lemma or unexpectedly decisive shortcut that arises from a newly inspected primary paper.
