## 1. Frame the inverse problem correctly

The researcher explicitly distinguishes *observations* labeled gravitational attraction, dark matter and dark energy from a particular **ontological explanation** of them. For astrophysics, report observed galaxy kinematics, lensing maps, cluster dynamics, CMB anisotropies, growth histories, BAO/supernova distance-redshift data and gravitational waves independently from the interpretation as nonbaryonic particles, a dark-energy fluid, or a modified gravitational law. Dark-matter particle candidates are not excluded a priori; equally, they are **not primordial constraints** on any causally emergent system.

The search aims for theoretical mechanisms which explain **why several apparently independent effects become correlated**. Agreement with one numerical observable is not internal-process equivalence. Preserve the previous strict model-selection requirements: specify source node/event semantics, true higher-order incidence, transformation composition, causal partial order, state/field assumptions and possible process/intervention preservation under an explicit map before claiming "same model." An entropy cone is an observable, not a microscopic update mechanism.

The user's speculative primitive entropy/back-pressure tendency is separate from physical thermodynamic entropy, holographic area law, superfluid chemical potential, von Neumann entropy, causal-set fluctuation model and an action written as quantum relative entropy.

## 2. Two independent axes for ranking candidates

The review distinguishes **fit to the requested causal ontology** from **fit to observed physics**, rather than compressing both into a deceptive one-number priority score.

- **S (structural proximity):** Is there a genuinely fundamental local relational transition, causal order, compositional dynamics, or precise emergence map that could be compared to an unbounded higher-order hypergraph *without assuming the desired bulk geometry*?
- **E (empirical/physical scope):** Does this framework derive or successfully fit independently calibrated observations on multiple scales, rather than one isolated galaxy or diagram?
- **A (assumption burden):** Does it impose external dimension, time, fixed valence, metric, fields, Hilbert/representation state, energy, particle population, chosen cosmological horizon, cutoff, or a phenomenological interpolation parameter?
- **X (falsifiability):** Can the model be rejected by crisp counterexamples: CMB peaks, lensing-gas mass offset, gravitational-wave speed, galaxy RAR residuals, evolving-expansion priors, conservation and high-order causal consistency?
- **P (potential common structural content):** Does it connect gravity to entropy, cosmological expansion to observer clocks, collective excitations to force laws, spacetime to entanglement, or causal order to continuum geometry with an actual derivation, not an analogy?

**Important:** A source may be high for structure but low for verified cosmology, or vice versa. "Priority A" in the registers means **read and compare first for research clues**, not a probability that the proposal describes the universe.

## 3. First-tier foundational candidates

### F01 — Rideout–Sorkin classical sequential growth and Sorkin's causal-set program

The [published classical sequential growth framework](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.61.024002) starts from a locally finite **partial order of events**, causality constraints and a discrete covariance principle to derive an admissible family of stochastic birth processes. This is the closest existing **model-construction comparison** to a primitive "causal step" without assuming Euclidean distance. It contains an intriguing comment that Ising-like variables on relations can represent potential matter emergence.

**Critical difference:** A locally finite binary relation poset is **not** an arbitrary higher-order incidence network; directed birth/ancestry cannot be identified with four-way causal interaction automatically. Quantum dynamics, particle spectra, physical Lorentzian metric and observed matter are **not** derived by this classical framework. Never infer a universal causal generator from a covariant family of growth models.

**Researchable piece:** General covariance/label invariance, Bell-causality-like consistency, per-event provenance, alternate scheduler independence, stable homology of order intervals. Compare exact definitions of independence of unrelated births to our multi-hyperedge rewrite semantics, without making pairwise links the entire model.

### F02 — Markopoulou quantum causal histories

[Quantum causal histories](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9904009) attach quantum state/Hilbert spaces to events and local unitary evolution consistent with the causal ordering. The related [insider's guide](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0920-5632(00)00791-X) connects observer-accessible local logic and causally evolving spin networks.

**Piece:** Explicit **event causal structure ↔ quantum information operations**. This is a particularly useful external process-fidelity test after the causally generated network supports a justified quantum-state map. **Gap:** the quantum degrees of freedom are inserted; the system does not derive Hilbert structure or the causal order from bare k-way rewriting.

### F03 — Gielen–Oriti–Sindoni group field theory condensates

In [*Cosmology from Group Field Theory Formalism for Quantum Gravity*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.031301), an interacting quantum-many-body **group field theory** has condensates interpreted as approximately homogeneous macroscopic spatial geometries, with a modified Friedmann equation in an isotropic WKB limit. The 2014 [continuation](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2014)013) develops the condensate construction; 2023 GFT analyses yield possible stationary and bounce regimes [PRD 108, 026001](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.026001).

**Piece:** A relatively explicit procedure for **micro-composition → collective phase → effective cosmological law**, exactly the missing kind of inside-the-model bridge. **Gap:** it postulates quantum/group-geometric quanta, gluing, chosen representation/rank and usually spatial dimension, and the cosmological approximations are not an experimental derivation of a real universe from nothing.

### F04 — Finster causal fermion systems

The [causal-action continuum framework](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-42067-7) and [gravity derivation review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05906) investigate how relativistic geometry, fermionic degrees and effective field equations may follow from a more abstract **measure/operator causal variational principle**.

**Piece:** A serious attempt to obtain **gravity and matter from a common deeper mathematical object**, unlike approaches that derive a modified gravitational force while taking particles for granted. **Gap:** the operator Hilbert structure, causal action, regularization and continuum hypotheses are assumed; it is **not** itself a purely combinatorial hypergraph. It deserves close formal comparison rather than being mistaken for empirical proof of the Standard Model.

### F05 — Jacobson 1995 thermodynamics and 2016 entanglement equilibrium

In [*Thermodynamics of Spacetime: The Einstein Equation of State*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.75.1260), the Einstein field equation is derived *conditionally* by applying a Clausius relation to all local causal/Rindler horizons, with area entropy and Unruh temperature. The 2016 [entanglement-equilibrium argument](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.201101) relates first-order Einstein equations and stationary vacuum entanglement under fixed-volume conditions in conformal quantum field settings.

**Piece:** Perhaps the cleanest **causal horizon + information/thermodynamic constraint → effective gravity** mathematical bridge. **Gap:** spacetime metric, local horizon, temperature/heat flux and/or geodesic-ball volume are imported rather than derived from a pre-metric causal network; this is not a microcausal evolution proof. Treat Einstein gravity as emergent *effective equation* within declared assumptions, not assume an entropy pressure drives our primitive rewrites.

## 4. Highest-leverage research on apparent dark matter

### D01 — Milgrom MOND and the measured radial acceleration relation

The original [MOND proposal](https://doi.org/10.1086/161130) and [McGaugh–Lelli–Schombert RAR](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.201101) establish an unusually economical link between baryon distributions and observed excess gravitational acceleration in many galaxies. This empirical regularity is a **strong benchmark** regardless of whether the right underlying explanation is MOND, baryon–halo coupling in dark matter, collective dark physics, or geometry.

**Piece:** A demand that apparently additional gravity on galaxy scales be a reproducible structural response rather than arbitrary new mass per galaxy. **Gap:** MOND is low-acceleration phenomenology, not an explanation of why the acceleration scale, general relativity, cluster offsets or CMB peaks arise.

### D02 — Skordis–Złośnik relativistic aether-scalar-tensor gravity (AeST)

[2021 PRL](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.127.161302) explicitly constructs a relativistic field theory admitting MOND-like galaxy acceleration and **observed CMB and linear matter power spectra** without conventional pressureless dark matter. A [2024 Hamiltonian analysis](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.110.044015) counts full nonlinear degrees of freedom and analyzes its constraint structure; separate works examine cluster solutions and stability.

**Piece:** Among modified-gravity competitors this offers a valuable **cross-scale consistency challenge** to naive dismissal of every no-particle-DM explanation. **Gap:** chosen Lorentzian metric, scalar and vector fields, field action, and additional cosmological degrees of freedom; no emergent primordial causal graph. Linear-scale fits are **not** a global validation of cluster lensing, nonlinear structure or every cosmological dataset.

### D03 — Erik Verlinde's emergent dark gravity and empirical stresses

The [2016 emergent gravity/dark universe preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02269) proposes that a de Sitter horizon's entanglement/thermal volume contribution and baryonic entropy displacement generate an additional apparent gravitational effect. The [2017 KiDS/GAMA isolated galaxy weak-lensing test](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/466/3/2547/2661916) found agreement in a restricted observational regime but required auxiliary cosmological/lensing assumptions.

**Falsification:** [Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2017](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnrasl/slx031) found the finite-size galaxy predictions generally required implausibly low stellar mass-to-light ratios and predicted RAR residual trends with radius not observed. A complete relativistic model reproducing cluster merger lensing/CMB has not been supplied by the original heuristic.

**Piece:** Strong explicit **dark energy-like horizon entropy ↔ apparent dark matter-like gravity** connection. **Gap:** assumes de Sitter cosmological horizon and the very baryonic particles/scale factors whose emergence we would need to explain. Mechanistic resemblance is not empirical success.

### D04 — Blanchet–Le Tiec gravitational polarization

[2008 PRD](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.78.024031) proposes a gravitationally polarizable **dipolar fluid**. The same covariant GR matter action was designed to give MOND-like effective attraction at galaxy scales and to be **linear-perturbatively indistinguishable** from a Λ plus cold dark matter cosmology. The [2009 follow-up](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.80.023524) develops its matter action; [2013/2014 second-order analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6991) identifies new nonlinear non-Gaussian signatures and Planck constraints.

**Piece:** Very high-value *unification-of-apparent-effects* idea: medium response, MOND-like attraction and an effective ΛCDM large-scale description arise from one constructed action. **Gap:** dipolar medium is still an additional physical ingredient; GR metric and fluid energy are input; strict derivation from a causal hypergraph would need a separate constructive map.

### D05 — Berezhiani–Khoury superfluid dark matter

[2015/2016 superfluid dark matter](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.92.103510) proposes strong-interaction particles forming a superfluid phase in galaxies, with collective **phonons causing MOND-like forces** and normal phases at cluster/cosmological scales.

**Piece:** Ordinary many-body **phase transition → emergent effective force** is concrete and computationally approachable. **Caution:** It proposes real dark matter particles. It does **not** answer the researcher's preferred question using *no dark matter entity*, but is still an excellent cross-field explanation for why a particle sector might mimic a modified gravitational law.

## 5. Highest-leverage research on apparent dark energy/expansion

### D06 — Buchert averaging and Wiltshire timescape

Buchert's [2000 averaged GR equations](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1001800617177) show that **averaging and evolving inhomogeneous geometry are not commuting operations**, leaving backreaction/curvature terms. Wiltshire's [timescape model](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.80.123512) interprets observational cosmic acceleration as possibly **apparent under different regional clock/rod calibrations** in inhomogeneous GR rather than new repulsive fluid.

**Piece:** A striking **observer/time-keeping and coarse-graining ↔ apparent dark-energy behavior** mechanism. Strong analogy for our question whether local clock chains and global causal topology produce observer-dependent effective expansion. **Gap:** assumes already geometric spacetime, regional time calibration and averaging prescriptions; fits are controversial. A [2025 Pantheon+ comparison](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnrasl/slae112) reports favorable timescape evidence under one pipeline, while independent BAO, CMB and supernova analyses are required before treating the model as preferred. June 2026 debate includes both skeptics and proponents of measured acceleration, not a settled reversal.

### D07 — Sorkin causal-set everpresent Λ

[A sustained stochastic Λ model in causal-set theory](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.87.063515) connects **fluctuating counts of discrete causal elements** and effective cosmic acceleration scale, rather than assuming a perfectly fixed vacuum-energy density.

**Piece:** Unusually close *causal relation/count* ↔ *effective dark-energy-like term*. **Warning:** [Barrow 2007](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.75.067301) points out sharp **CMB isotropy** constraints on its stochastic fluctuations. Do not selectively cite the claimed matching order of magnitude without reviewing observational defeat scenarios. The construction assumes cosmological quantum/gravitational background relations and model-specific fluctuations.

### D08 — Deser–Woodard nonlocal gravity

[2007 PRL](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.99.111301) introduces modifications inspired by quantum-loop **nonlocal gravitational memory**, designed to have negligible local effects but influence cosmological acceleration through accumulated history.

**Piece:** Possibly helpful if cosmic effective forces reflect nonlocal causal-history constraints. **Gap:** Lorentzian curvature and a specific nonlocal inverse-operator/retarded prescription are already given; whether the model meets CMB, growth and GW constraints depends on precise variants. Nonlocality alone does not imply pregeometry.

### D09 — f(Q), f(R), tracker quintessence and holographic DE

- [f(Q) 2026 observational models](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jheap.2026.100609) use the geometric **nonmetricity** scalar to obtain effective accelerated expansion. [f(R) review](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.82.451) studies curvature functional extensions and their relationships to scalar–tensor theories. These are valuable for *exact alternative-formulation equivalence analysis* but start with spacetime metric/connection.
- [Quintessence tracker fields](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.82.896) offer an attracting scalar-field evolution and explanation for an effective near-present cosmic-density coincidence. They add scalar field and potential, not solve pre-spatial causal origin.
- [Li's holographic dark-energy model](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2004.10.014) ties cosmological density to a horizon/IR–UV constraint, but assumes an event horizon and cosmic scale, not a primitive causal step.

These are serious *phenomenological effective targets*, generally weaker sources of information about **what primitive k-way causal update actually happens**.

## 6. Other foundational leads not to lose

- [Causal dynamical triangulations](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.100.091304): numerical emergent de Sitter-like large-scale geometry **within a dimension-fixed triangulation ensemble**. Highly instructive phase/scale controls, but dimensionality was **selected** in the setup (avoid earlier Model A's artificial 12D cap).
- [Loop quantum gravity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrr-2008-5) and [spin foams](https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrr-2013-3): graph-local quantum geometry and evolution amplitudes, already supply spin labels and gravity algebra.
- [Quantum graphity](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.77.104029): Hamiltonian graph phases can favor low-energy connectivity/locality but impose a fixed number of nodes, energy, chosen interaction law and pairwise graphs.
- [Levin–Wen string-net condensation](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.71.045110): concrete quantum many-body emergence of gauge and fermionic excitations, but on a supplied lattice and quantum Hilbert structure.
- [Causal fermion systems](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-42067-7) and [Chamseddine–Connes spectral action](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.77.4868) look for one deeper mathematical description of matter and gravity; the former uses a Hilbert/operator causal variational principle, the latter inputs spectral geometry and chosen Standard Model algebra. Both are **fundamental physics research**, and high priority for comparing how matter-like information could be represented without importing it into the source generator.
- [Asymptotic safety](https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrr-2006-5): RG flow and UV fixed-point/scale-dependent dimensions constrain quantum gravitational continuum theories, but presuppose metric effective action and depend on truncation.
- [Tensor-network holographic error correction](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2015)149) and [Swingle's emergent geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.3304): map entanglement structure to effective geometric bulk; Hilbert space, tensor network and boundary are specified, not derived from k-way bare causal rewriting.
- [Padmanabhan emergent cosmic expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4916): cosmological growth driven by bulk/surface degree-of-freedom imbalance, assumes existing Hubble volume, horizon, temperature and clock.
- [CST causal-order topology recovery](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.2435599) is an essential comparator for topology as an observable of causal precedence under exact assumptions. Do not replace true higher-order participation by a mere poset unless mapping losses are audited.

### Mechanism bridges to extract, without importing their ontology

1. **Compositional causation:** local high-order rewrite provenance and label invariance (CSG, QCH).
2. **Collective phase/condensation:** stable macroscopic regimes emerge from constrained interacting constituents (GFT, string nets, superfluid analogue).
3. **Constitutive response versus microforce:** gravitational equations as effective horizon information/thermodynamic constraints (Jacobson, Verlinde).
4. **Observer/averaging mismatch:** locally valid geometry/clock observations produce a different globally inferred expansion (Buchert/timescape).
5. **Nonlocal memory:** accumulated causal history changes an effective large-scale force (Deser–Woodard; a possible mathematical analogy to k-way causal histories).
6. **Apparent missing mass via response/polarization:** galaxy-scale effective attraction and large-scale Λ-like behavior from one constructed medium (AeST/dipolar).
7. **Strong structural consistency:** the same model must preserve actual event/intervention mechanism where a correspondence is claimed, not just a shared graph exponent, scalar entropy or final galaxy rotation curve.

## 9. Proposed economical falsification pipeline

**Stage 1: provenance and assumption ledger.** For each short-listed paper freeze source exact statement, primitive objects (order, labels, metric, fields, energy, boundary), causal/update rules, limiting approximations and proven/no-go theorems. Never mix inferred phenomena with proposed ontology.

**Stage 2: internal equivalence gates.** Compare what an operation actually does: which k-way relations it changes, what causal event precedes what, how states compose, which observed changes persist under node relabeling and update scheduling. A strictly pairwise/metric Hamiltonian cannot be promoted into a pre-metric k-ary primitive without an explicit representation map and loss audit.

**Stage 3: minimal executable witness.** Pick one specific prediction per candidate (e.g. covariance across k-ary birth schedules, robust coarse-graining/phase pattern, operational boundary reconstruction, observer-clock-related volume effect). Provide its counterexample/null: tree exponents, fixed dimension, fake pairwise 2-section, scripted node-birth persistence, entropy-cut noninjectivity, hardcoded cosmological acceleration scale, artificially bounded tensor rank.

**Stage 4: cross-scale observational burden.** Require distinct measured phenomena independently: galaxies, collision lensing, early CMB, large-scale growth, expansion BAO/SN and GW signal consistency, with observational systematics. No single MOND curve or dark-energy w fit is evidence of completeness.

**Stage 5: abstain from premature "unified theory" ranking.** There is no empirical proof that any listed pregeometry program produces both the Standard Model and ΛCDM observables without adding assumptions. The shortlist prioritizes **reusable, potentially load-bearing pieces**, not a favorite answer about the world's ultimate cause.

## 10. Findings, limits and provenance

**Most concrete intersection found:** Rideout–Sorkin CSG supplies causal-growth consistency; Markopoulou QCH supplies relational quantum process; GFT provides a constructive many-body mechanism for effective cosmology; Jacobson conditional causal-horizon thermodynamics demonstrates an entropy–Einstein link; AeST/dipolar/Verlinde supply *distinct* attempts at apparent dark-matter physics; Buchert/timescape and causal-set Λ supply competing explanations for apparent cosmic acceleration. These connections must be tested for **structural invariants**, not simply collated as compatible.

**Most important negatives:** CDT and much simplicial quantum geometry choose dimension `d` in advance; Verlinde’s original dark gravity does not universally pass galaxy RAR; casual stochastic Λ may violate CMB isotropy; a galaxy-fitted MOND force is not enough for Bullet Cluster CMB; baryonic radial correlation does not disprove dark particles; observer-calibration explanations depend on measured survey consistency; no entropic horizon formalism directly specifies a fundamental k-ary rewrite engine.
