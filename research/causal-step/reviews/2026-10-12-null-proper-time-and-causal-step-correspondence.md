## 1. What special relativity actually establishes

In flat spacetime, with conventional physical constant `c`, the invariant differential interval is

```
(c dτ)^2 = (c dt)^2 − dx^2 − dy^2 − dz^2
```

for timelike paths where proper time is the carried-clock duration. Massless light in geometric optics travels on a **null curve** where

```
ds^2 = 0, hence integrated proper-time length = 0.
```

A room-spanning light path and a long astronomical light path can **both** be null, even though clocks in an observer's frame assign different nonzero travel durations. This is an invariant distinction between **path type** and observer-dependent time/distance measurements.

**Do not say the photon has its own rest-frame viewpoint or literally experiences zero seconds.** Photons have no inertial rest frame; proper time is not a usable monotonic worldline parameter for them. The precise result is that the metric proper-time length of a null curve is zero, not that emission and detection become the same event or a photon observes its whole path instantaneously.

Citations for standard relativity:
- [University of Toronto lecture notes on general relativity, Chapter 1](https://www.math.toronto.edu/aretakis/General%20Relativity-Aretakis.pdf): timelike worldlines for observers, null geodesics for photons and no proper-time parametrization of photon paths.
- [Sean Carroll, Lecture Notes on General Relativity](https://www.physics.smu.edu/scalise/P3368sp17/CarrollGR.pdf): proper time and null intervals.
- [University at Buffalo, Special Relativity Boot Camp](https://www.acsu.buffalo.edu/~whkinney/SpecialRelativityBootCamp/): clock proper time and null classification.

These conventional time and distance quantities belong to the comparison theory, **not** to the project's primitive causal-time definition.

## 2. One causal link versus observer causal-event sequences

The researcher's conjecture can be represented as a candidate graph interpretation:

- `e`: emission event.
- `d`: detection event.
- `L(e,d)`: a *proposed* direct light-causal relation, regarded as one unit in a candidate representation.
- `O_0 ≺ O_1 ≺ ... ≺ O_N`: another system's sequence of causally dependent clock/observer events.

A light signal may connect a near source to a detector or a distant source to a detector with the **same link count of one**, while the observer's experimentally established relations to surrounding events have very different clock-event histories.

However, neither a **null curve** nor a single source-to-detector causal influence is automatically a **cover relation** or elementary physical rewriting event. A mathematical null ray can contain arbitrary intermediate points/events along the ray, even though each segment also has zero proper-time length. It is consistent with the researcher's hypothesis to *choose* a representation collapsing an uninterrupted transmission into one edge; the collapse must later be justified under an explicit physical criterion.

**Crucial structural correction:** The endpoints of a strictly null-separated signal **cannot also be connected by a positive-proper-time timelike path** in standard Lorentzian spacetime. Therefore one must not casually draw an independent longer *timelike* clock chain from the **exact same emission event to the exact same detection event** if those endpoints are supposed to be null-separated. The proper two-path comparison is a **radar round trip**:

```
Observer sends (S) --null light signal--> reflector (M)
Reflector (M) --null return signal--> observer receives (R)

Observer clock: S -> tick1 -> tick2 -> ... -> tickN -> R
```

`S` and `R` lie on the observer's own timelike trajectory; `M` is an event away from that worldline. Two null links can coexist with the observer accumulating `N` causally successive clock events **between S and R**. Near versus far reflector experiments differ in `N` without introducing a pre-existing primitive second or unit of length.

These clock events count local physical changes. They are not guaranteed to match conventional elapsed proper time without an additional bridge law.

## 3. Why the distinction could matter for relativity's emergence

Ordinary special relativity predicts that different massive observers can accumulate different proper times along different timelike histories, while null causal relations and their lightcone character are preserved. The project might attempt to derive the macroscopic analogue of those properties from a structured causal-event system.

A candidate **reconstruction program** is:

1. Specify elementary causal event/step semantics *without* defining spatial distance or elapsed conventional time.
2. Identify signal-propagation/null-type relations and local repeatable clock-event sequences from that primitive structure.
3. Recover a consistent emergent notion of clock rate and spatial distance from comparisons among causal histories.
4. Test whether the resulting effective geometry satisfies Lorentz invariance, time dilation, relativity of simultaneity, and an invariant signal cone. If cosmology or gravitation is included, also test curved/expanding-spacetime phenomena.

**The important open question is how event-chain relations acquire a metric.** Merely having different numbers of causal events is not sufficient to reproduce the Lorentz factor or the full symmetry group of special relativity.

In causal-set theory, the timelike metric duration is related—under stringent assumptions about a faithful embedding and discretization—to **longest chains of causal elements**, not generic shortest unweighted graph paths. Null-related, timelike and spacelike relationships are distinct. This provides adjacent mathematics but does not validate the user's physical proposal:
[Sumati Surya, The Causal Set Approach to Quantum Gravity (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41114-019-0023-1).

## 4. Disambiguating the researcher's “one step”

Three separate notions remain possible:

1. **A direct causal relation:** emission influences detection, regardless of emergent distance.
2. **One microscopic rewrite/update:** a particular irreducible transition of the generative rules.
3. **A lightlike interval of zero proper time:** a standard invariant of relativistic metric geometry.

These are **not mathematically equivalent by definition**. The author is exploring whether a single physical principle can connect them; establishing the connection is a research goal, not a given.

A direct connection between e and d retains their identity as **two distinct causally ordered events**. Zero proper time does not mean no events, no causal succession, or no physical propagation.

## 5. Assessment

**Defensible observation:** Light and massive-observer clocks have sharply different relativistic proper-time behavior; the *null versus timelike* distinction remains even when both a distant and a nearby light path have zero interval.

**Promising hypothesis:** The user's causal-step language might represent the distinction between direct causal influence and longer observer-local causal-event sequences, providing the beginnings of a relational account of measured duration and distance.

**Not established:** That all uninterrupted light transmissions are one primitive causal update, that any light quantum has a rest-frame perspective or experiences one tick, or that special relativity follows from event-chain counts alone.
