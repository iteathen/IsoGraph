## 1. Researcher's boundary and intent

The researcher has seen only a small part of Ginestra Bianconi's topology research, and specifically **does not assert that her entire mathematical program is simple or trivial**. Her work remains an interesting distant lead, not an adequate basis by itself for searching for physical patterns in a proposed **unbounded hypergraph**.

The researcher requires substantial, well-founded topology, explicit node structures, and **objective empirical evidence** before giving substantial weight to formally publishing a *causal-graph/topology connection*. The distinction is about **readiness of the connection**, not a prohibition on continuing privately recorded foundational research, theoretical derivation, benchmark construction, or negative-result preservation.

The terms **“unbounded hypergraph”**, “node”, “event”, “hyperedge”, “topology”, and “physical dimension” are *not yet frozen mathematical definitions* for Causal Step. In particular, “unbounded” might mean no prescribed maximal hyperedge arity, maximal order/coordinate dimension, number of events, or observation horizon; these cannot be silently equated. Any future formal claim must explicitly choose its scope. Resource-limited simulators approximate candidate structures and cannot establish infinite-dimensional/unbounded behavior by execution alone.

**Source-based nuance:** It is inaccurate to generalize that Bianconi's work uses only “simple topology.” She has a serious and differentiated higher-order network/topology research program. **Her work is nevertheless not an independently verified map onto the particular unbounded causal hypergraph we are trying to formulate.** Nor do these papers prove the human research hypothesis that a maximally simple primordial generator is the actual origin of all observed complexity.

In particular: an **unrestricted hypergraph** may have incidence relations on arbitrary subsets of nodes without requiring every proper subset as a relation. A **simplicial complex** is closed under faces, and a **cell complex** additionally includes specified cell attachments and boundary incidence. A **causal relation** requires direction/precedence semantics. Conversions between these formalisms may be lossy or require additional weights/annotations, depending on the exact target; do not assume every representation is equivalent or incommensurable. Bianconi's 2022 weighted construction is an actual representation-power result, not a casual picture, and deserves source-faithful analysis **when our target semantics are defined**.

## 3. Staged internal research gate (not a formal publication draft)

### G0 — Define the proposed source structure

Specify the node/event identity regime, adjacency versus event precedence, multiple-place incidence, ordered or unordered hyperedges, admissible arity/rank, update/transition semantics, and whether “unbounded” refers to cardinality, arity, structural/physical dimension, or the absence of hard ontology bounds. Separate operational simulation resource ceilings from model-law limits. Any rule that fixes the answer (e.g. prechosen `d`) must be marked as an input.

**Failure criterion:** source graph can only be described by a picture, guessed dimensional label or undisclosed representation assumptions.

### G1 — Topological and structural validity

Choose exactly which invariants matter (examples: boundary operators with `∂²=0` where cells are actually defined, order complexes with explicitly declared causal-to-simplicial construction, incidence homology, Betti/persistent invariants, spectral structure, dimension estimators, causal reachability). Establish mathematics appropriate to the chosen representation; state equivalence criteria and what a particular encoding forgets or adds.

Require proofs *for formal mathematical claims* and counterexamples/negative controls. A single possible derived topology is not canonical unless its uniqueness/invariance is actually demonstrated. A generic cycle in an undirected graph is **not automatically a filled 2-cell**.

**Failure criterion:** relabeling, alternative encoding or conservative change of sampling changes the claimed invariant without disclosed dependence.

### G2 — Unboundedness and finite-resource independence

No finite simulation proves a universal law of an unbounded family. Provide a **generating/recursion law and structural bounds or a theorem** for the claimed unbounded behavior (where possible), with transparent finite approximation and error/scale analysis. Run independent resource ceilings and topology/event-generation sources; preserve sparse/late breakdown cases. Prior Model A evidence includes a false 12-dimensional cap and graph-hop exponents near 3 on a simple tree, demonstrating that finite numeric coincidences can be misleading.

**Failure criterion:** an observed fixed-dimensional, “scale-free” or universal pattern disappears when a budget, update horizon, chosen topology or measure is varied.

### G3 — Objective evidence and falsification

Distinguish three warrant types:
- **Formal mathematics:** correct stated theorem/derivation conditional on explicit assumptions.
- **Reproducible computational evidence:** independent implementations, public inputs, tests and negative controls demonstrating behavior of a specified finite model.
- **Empirical physical evidence:** independently measured, real-world observables or testable predictions that distinguish this causal/topological model from relevant alternatives, together with a fully defined observation map and uncertainty analysis.

A published model of collaboration/brain networks can supply **domain-specific empirical evidence of network methods**, not physics evidence for emergent spacetime. The researcher's demand for objective empirical proof is interpreted as a **strong independent-evidence burden**, not as a claim that finite experiments constitute a universal mathematical proof.

**Failure criterion:** physical agreement rests only on fitting, visual appearance, or a quantity already built into the update rule.

### G4 — Publication disposition

Keep internal comparison and methodological research ongoing. Mark **any specific publication asserting a causal-graph/topology correspondence as not ready** until G0–G3 evidence appropriate to that claim exists. A future author decision and applicable IsoGraph provenance, complete-comparison and qualification policies remain separately required. No public-facing manuscript, qualified semantic promotion or implied physical confirmation is authorized by this literature lead.

## 4. Concrete research sequence after this gate is accepted

The next defensible internal steps concern our **own topology and node semantics**, not retrofitting Bianconi's entropy action into a simulation:

1. Enumerate competing formal source representations (directed event DAG / causal order, arbitrary hypergraph, ordered incidence structures, simplicial completion, cell complex) while keeping their extra assumptions explicit.
2. Compare what each represents and loses, particularly multidirectional interactions, higher-order incidence, time-as-causal-succession, and possible unbounded arity. Keep equivalence **scope-specific**.
3. Build tiny exact adversarial examples with known invariants and ambiguity, then test complete representations and generation rules.
4. Only after G0/G1 are mature, consider Bianconi's network geometry papers as **potential external comparison targets**, and keep the currently unfounded gravitational connection separate.
