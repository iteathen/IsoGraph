## Purpose and scope

This directory reserves the research project name **Causal Step**, as requested by Joshua Oshiro.

The title now has a **researcher-specified terminological meaning**: **causal step** is the newly defined unit the researcher intends when using the term *light speed* in this inquiry. This is **not** the Einsteinian speed of light or a unit derived from conventional relativistic distance/time. The exact mathematical operations and any possible relationship to observable propagation have **not** yet been specified. Do not impose existing spacetime or velocity semantics.

This is a separate research folder. Its creation does not merge it into Minimal Rule, Minimum Representation, Entropy and Indistinguishability, or any other existing research topic.

## Working premises — established for investigation, not proved

1. **Emergent reality (Premise 1).** Reality is **largely an emergent effect from a simple set of conditions**.
2. **Causal step as the newly defined light-speed unit (Premise 2).** The researcher explicitly **redefines “light speed” as a new unit called “causal step.”** In this inquiry, **one unit of this redefined “light speed” is one causal step**. The original statement that light speed is *likely* a single causal step remains a provisional physical hypothesis, while the **unit's chosen meaning is a deliberate definition**, not an inference from established relativity.

**Epistemic status:** Premise 1 is provisional. Premise 2 contains a **stipulated new unit definition** (*causal step*) and a provisional suggestion about causal-step-based reality. Neither supplies experimental evidence or a derivation of actual physical laws. **Terminological separation is mandatory:** do not identify this unit with the conventional physical constant `c`, Einsteinian kinematics, distance per time, the Planck scale, a photon path, or a discretized spacetime interval. No further physical premises or quantitative rules are adopted.

## Explicit definition of time — causal succession (2026-10-12)

**The researcher defines time as the sequence of causal events.** In *Causal Step*, time does **not** mean a pre-existing duration, clock parameter, seconds, minutes, hours, days, or other conventional temporal measurement. A causal step belongs to a sequence or connection of causal occurrences, not a unit of Einsteinian light speed and not a fixed duration of conventional time.

Accordingly:
- **“Earlier” / “later”** mean relations of causal precedence or succession, not readings from an external clock.
- **“Beginning”** means a first causal event/condition or a minimal causal starting relation **if one exists**, not a point at conventional time zero.
- **“Multiple beginnings” or “simultaneous possibilities”** may mean causally independent branches with no predecessor relationship between them; do **not** import a universal clock to interpret the researcher's use of “simultaneous.”
- The researcher has specified that **time is the sequence of causal events**, but has **not** yet required that every event belong to one global total order. A branching or partial causal order remains an *optional mathematical representation* to assess, not an additional premise.

This is an **explicit terminology correction to prior assistant reasoning**, not a new physical hypothesis, a measured time scale, or a modification of the two working premises. When analyzing future origin models, reason in terms of causal succession first, and discuss conventional spacetime only as a separately emergent or comparative description.

## First substantive research question — causal origin before space

The researcher now asks how a simple set of conditions could generate reality **without presupposing physical space**, and whether the relevant causal beginning is unique, multiply rooted, repeated without bound, or better understood as an **atemporal space of possible histories**. A Ruliad-like all-possibilities model and observer-conditioned access to connected or persisting portions of it are **hypotheses and comparisons**, not yet adopted physical premises.

**Central distinction (clarified):** A **causal source / earliest event** may be a fully meaningful **causal center**, defined by common ancestry, even before space exists. It is not automatically a **spatial center** of a later three-dimensional universe. If no space exists initially, the primitive cause has no spatial “where.” Likewise, the researcher's “all beginning simultaneously” must be read in the causal-step sense: **multiple histories may begin without any causal precedence between them**. No conventional synchronized timestamps are assumed. Whether incomparable events form a useful formal notion of contemporaneity is an open modeling question.

**Three-dimensionality remains to be derived.** A possible mathematical test is whether sufficiently large intrinsic balls of radius r in an **emergent spatial adjacency graph**, not merely in the causal event graph or Ruliad multiway graph, contain a number of accessible nodes proportional to r cubed. Arbitrary branching does not guarantee this: a regular tree has exponentially increasing volume. The requirements for stable structures, observer access, and effective matter remain to be specified separately.

**Matter near an origin?** A first causal event by itself does **not** require a density maximum around a position in present-day space. A particular model may introduce a persistent preferred spatial location or an initial density gradient, but then the effect is a **model-dependent testable consequence**, not an implication of the two premises alone. Ordinary Big Bang cosmology also distinguishes early dense conditions from an explosion at one center.

**Competing origin structures under review:** single minimal causal condition, multiple minimal causal conditions, no privileged first cause, and atemporal totality of possible computations. These are **candidate formulations rather than third/fourth/etc. premises**. The compatibility of the Wolfram Ruliad proposal with the new causal-step unit has not been shown.

## Causal center and the near/far light-link puzzle

**Causal origin and time, as the researcher intends them:** A shared earliest causal condition **can be a causal center**—a root defined by ancestry, despite there being no pre-existing physical coordinates. A first causal event can equally be a beginning in **causal time**, since time means the sequence of causal events. Neither idea requires a conventional spatial center or clock-time origin. The previous analysis was too restrictive when it treated the absence of space as a reason to deny *any* center.

**New research example:** The investigator proposes counting a single uninterrupted light transmission from emitter to observer as **one causal step**, both for a nearby lamp and for a very distant astronomical source. These are candidate *direct causal light links*, regardless of the observed geometric separation. **This is a proposed interpretation, not yet a derived fundamental update rule.**

**Possible reconciliation:** The number of direct light links is not necessarily the same observable as the distance or event-history length measured using other parts of the causal network. A two-way signal experiment can have two light connections—send-to-reflector and reflector-to-receiver—while the observer's **local causal-clock chain** between sending and receiving contains small or large numbers of tick events depending on the experiment. Those are *causal sequences*, not conventional seconds imported as primitives. Deriving a spatial distance from these relations still requires a defined invariant mapping; shortest unweighted path counting across arbitrary light links would incorrectly collapse near/far separation.

**Relativity as a correspondence, not the unit definition:** Standard **special relativity** treats unobstructed lightlike connections as *null* regardless of spatial travel distance, although a zero spacetime interval does **not** establish that the signal took one elementary graph update. **General relativity** extends the macroscopic account to curved and cosmologically expanding geometry, including different redshifts and distance measures. Conventional constant `c` and metric time appear only within these proposed **emergent comparison theories**; they are not synonymous with the user's primitive causal-step unit.

The precise link semantics and emergent distance map remain open. These are questions for comparative model research, not additional adopted premises.

## Lightlike paths and observer causal time — current comparison

**Conditional investigation based on the adopted causal-step premise:** The researcher asks whether light's relativistic *null* behavior might provide a bridge between a direct emission-to-detection causal step and the different rates at which observers accumulate causally successive events. The premise is accepted as the starting assumption of this research, not repeatedly challenged for lack of proof.

In standard relativity, **null paths have zero metric proper-time length**, whereas an observer's **timelike** path carries a clock whose proper time accumulates. Light has no rest frame, so this is a geometric statement, not a claim about what a photon subjectively experiences. In the proposed Causal Step interpretation, an emission-to-detection *direct causal link* might coexist with many causally ordered events recorded by a physical observer. A properly formed two-way light-signal comparison makes this compatible with separate observer histories.

**Open structural target:** determine whether a chosen causal-event graph can produce not only different event counts but the quantitative relative clock-rate relationships of special relativity, while preserving the project's original meanings of **causal step** and **time as causal succession**. This is a **deduction/search target**, not a third premise.

## Executed supporting toy model — Model A Dimension Birth

The author's historical **[Supplementary Model A — Dimension Birth](https://github.com/iteathen/IsoGraph/blob/research/import-archived-papers-20261012/research/primitive-presentation/evidence/source/supplementary/model-a-dimension-birth.html)** (original archived under Primitive Presentation, source-import PR #75) has now been **actually executed locally**, not merely read. It is a useful **supporting toy model**, not the Causal Step theory itself and not a theorem. The historical source has been preserved byte-for-byte.

- [2026-10-12 reproducible computational/visual audit and comparative source test](reviews/2026-10-12-model-a-execution-audit.md) — numerical checkpoints to **2,500** model steps, actual browser renderer screenshots and animation, source review, and a reproducible defect.
- [Verbatim request and complete attribution of the results](DISCUSSION_LOG.md).

**Verified observations:** Fresh default runs produce **533 nodes, 682 edges, and 10 internal dimensions by step 200**; **1,321 nodes / 1,705 edges / 12 dimensions by step 500**; and **2,599 nodes / 3,364 edges by step 1,000**. A dense connected central structure emerges visually as graph size increases. Coordinate dimensions are introduced at steps **1, 22, ..., 232**, reaching the programmed **12-dimensional cap**; the browser renderer plots only the first three dimensions. A hard **3,000-node cap and older-node pruning** create a distinct later regime: the graph fragments into **33 components by step 1,250** and **209 by step 2,500**. Do not confuse this implementation-driven fragmentation with established physics.

**Reproducible defect:** Pressing Reset duplicates the starting node because `reset()` pushes the return value of `makeNode()`, which already adds the node. The resulting graph is not identical to a fresh-run graph. The source remains unmodified; a separately labeled correction/control layer would be required before treating reset trajectories as comparable.

**Research interpretation:** The run supplies concrete evidence of how **the program's own chosen rules** produce branching, central concentration, extended coordinate structure and late graph fragmentation. It **does not yet derive** the Causal Step unit, causal-event time, lightlike propagation, intrinsic 3D dimensionality or relativistic clock rates. Its input source is deterministic hash/optional Rule 30, not an actual stochastic quantum source. Treat it as a computational test substrate for further causality hypotheses and independently defined geometry measurements.

## Uncapped dimension-control experiment — October 12, 2026

The researcher expressly **withdrew** an apparent 12-dimensional coincidence with Lisi/Woit after confirming that the archived Model A has an explicit 12-dimension ceiling. The researcher then requested: **“We need to uncap the dimensions to see if it stabilizes.”** The separate controlled experiment changed only `internalDimCap: 12` to `internalDimCap: Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY`; historical source and all other original model rules remained unchanged.

**Executed finding:** Dimensions continue beyond twelve: **15 at step 300, 24 at 500, 48 at 1,000, 72 at 1,500, 96 at 2,000 and 120 at 2,500**, with no plateau. Rule 30 input gives the same 48 dimensions at step 1,000 despite different graph topology. All 120 observed dimension births occur at `step = 1+21*(dimension−1)`. The cause is **code-driven**: each newborn initially shares the parent's coordinates yet receives a unit-length edge, triggering the maximum-error test, while the 20-step cooldown permits another dimension every 21 updates. This archived model therefore **does not self-select a fixed spatial dimensionality** after uncapping.

**Independent orbital-motion control:** Projected circulation persists. In the same 107-node cohort, **64 uncapped versus 65 capped** trajectories completed more than one net x–y revolution relative to the evolving center from steps 200–950. This does not establish closed, persistent bound orbits in the full high-dimensional system.

## Extended Causal Step Model A controls — 3,000 steps (2026-10-12)

An independently transcribed **source-dynamics Node 22** experiment reproduced the original capped and uncapped step-1,000 checkpoints and cohort windings. The uncapped run continued to **143 internal dimensions at step 3,000**, with births strictly at steps `1+21*(d−1)`, no detected intrinsic plateau and no nonfinite coordinates/velocities at sampled checkpoints. At step 3,000 the default node ceiling was active: **2,994 nodes, 3,056 edges, 227 connected components**.

**Separately controlled interventions:** eliminating only the 3,000-node ceiling produced **3,471 nodes, 72 dimensions and one connected component at step 1,500**; setting only the spring constant to zero left **46 dimensions by step 950** but **0/107 projected cohort trajectories** with >1 x–y revolution (compared with **64/107** with springs). This supports different causal mechanisms for dimensions, fragmentation, and circulation.

A separate continued-cohort measurement found that **46 of 93 original nodes surviving steps 950–1,200** completed more than one *additional* projected x–y revolution during that interval. Among the initial 107 nodes, only 21 remained at step 1,500 and none at 1,750 due to programmed pruning/truncation. This is persistent circulation in the model, not permanent orbital binding.\n\nThe 64 projected orbit-like trajectories did **not** demonstrate stable full-dimensional orbits: their median full-space radial coefficient of variation is ≈0.304, no >1-winding trajectory in the cohort has full-space radial CV below 0.1, and first-two-coordinate radius is only about 24.3% of full-space *squared* radius at step 950 (cohort median). A radius-exclusion robustness check still yielded **58/107** >1 windings, supporting real projected circulation without proving gravitational binding.
