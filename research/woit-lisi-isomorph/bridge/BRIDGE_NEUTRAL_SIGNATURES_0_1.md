## B01 — chiral action / incidence

Native IDs 187001–187009.

Weak signature 187001 contains:
- a vector-like carrier;
- a spinor-like carrier;
- two distinguished chiral subcarriers;
- an action object;
- a grading object;
- the action is typed from vector + spinor back to spinor;
- the action exchanges the two chiral subcarriers.

Strong refinement 187008 adds 187009, which is explicitly linked as a derived view of reusable Clifford-module schema 185004.

This creates the intended nesting:

~~~text
typed chiral action
    <- full Clifford-action refinement
~~~

A future source projection may instantiate the weak signature without instantiating the strong refinement.
