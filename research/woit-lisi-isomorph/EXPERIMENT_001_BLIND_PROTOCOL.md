# Experiment 001 — Blind Primitive Common-Quotient Protocol

**Status:** PREREGISTERED / NOT YET EXECUTED TO CLOSURE  
**Corpus:** `SOURCE_CORPUS_FREEZE_0_1.md`

## Objective

Determine whether independently rendered source structures (W) and (L) contain a nontrivial common quotient under a declared structural view, without using the proposed bridge to construct either rendering.

## Phase 0 — source isolation

- Renderer W receives only W01–W04 plus current IsoGraph authority.
- Renderer L receives only L01–L05 plus current IsoGraph authority.
- Neither renderer receives `HYPOTHESIS_QUARANTINE_0_1.md`.
- O01 is withheld until the obstruction-audit phase.
- Cross-track terminology is forbidden as a rendering shortcut.

## Phase 1 — Source Semantic Census

For each track:

1. traverse every frozen source;
2. assign stable census IDs to every load-bearing assertion, definition, operator/relation semantic, guard, binding/scope obligation, transition, reality/chirality condition, representation assignment, and material unknown;
3. preserve conflicts between source revisions rather than normalizing them away;
4. mark mutable/work-in-progress statements as such;
5. freeze the completed SSC before strict primitive closure begins.

Current `TRACK_*_WORKING_SSC_0_1.md` files are shells, not completed censuses.

## Phase 2 — primitive rendering

Reduce every in-scope census item until it reaches:

- primitive logic;
- exact raw incidence/data;
- exact schema-generating semantics; or
- a qualified QU boundary.

Familiar labels such as twistor, chirality, gauge field, root, Clifford algebra, CPT, triality, real form, or (E_8) may remain as provenance/display names but may not serve as opaque semantic leaves when their behavior is load-bearing.

## Phase 3 — recursive implicit assertions

Run recursive IA discovery independently for each track until the pinned operational fixed point is reached.

The IA profile must be recorded. Any change to primitive support, scope, QU state, authority, or profile invalidates the fixed point and reopens closure.

## Phase 3.5 — intra-author formulation families

Before any W-to-L comparison, construct a formulation graph separately inside each track.

For every candidate pair of formulations within one author's corpus:

1. identify whether they purport to express the same or overlapping idea;
2. test the relation from primitive structure rather than terminology;
3. classify the relation only to the strength actually supported;
4. preserve a transformation/reconstruction witness where available;
5. preserve all residual structure that prevents stronger equivalence;
6. record directionality when one formulation reconstructs from another but not conversely.

Possible bookkeeping relations include:

- exact equivalence under the pinned scope;
- derivable/scoped equivalence;
- alternative factorization or coordinate/representation change;
- specialization/generalization;
- real-form/signature/chirality realization;
- revision/supersession;
- analogy without proved equivalence;
- unresolved relation.

These labels do not add Core primitives.

Each resulting concept/formulation family remains a graph of related representations; it must not be collapsed into one canonical node merely for comparison convenience.

### Bridge-relative representative selection

After the intra-author graphs are sealed, DP 0.9 may be used to evaluate which representative exposes a proposed cross-domain correspondence with the least unsupported structure while preserving reconstruction.

The evaluation profile must include at least:

- exact reconstruction back to the source formulation;
- size/nature of cross-domain residuals;
- dependence on QU or unproved assumptions;
- robustness across alternate formulations in the same family;
- preservation of semantic role and transition anatomy;
- directionality/information loss.

No representative may be selected because it makes the expected bridge easier. Selection is an evidence result, not a preprocessing assumption.

## Phase 4 — seal and blind

Before comparison:

- verify SSC conservation;
- freeze graph hashes/revisions;
- freeze IA ledgers;
- freeze intra-author formulation graphs and all claimed transform witnesses;
- replace domain-facing labels with neutral handles in the comparison projection;
- preserve source-facing reconstruction maps outside the blinded view.

Blind relabeling occurs **after** source-faithful closure, not before.

## Phase 5 — common-quotient search

Use DP to search for:

- exact structural isomorphisms;
- scoped common cores;
- residual differences;
- alternative factorizations;
- family-to-family bridges across all eligible formulation representatives;
- asymmetric translation/reconstruction paths;
- minimum sufficient support for each proposed correspondence.

Use NEI only for natural/domain identity questions actually required by a candidate mapping. Same structural role is not automatically NEI SAME.

Use DTS when the mapping depends on transitions such as real-form selection, signature change, symmetry breaking, conjugation/chirality exchange, or representation reassignment.

## Falsifier-first requirements

For every admitted mapping, attempt at least:

- role-swap falsifier;
- reality-condition/dimension falsifier;
- chirality/conjugation falsifier;
- transition-anatomy falsifier when DTS is involved;
- removal test for (E_8)-specific support;
- reconstruction test back to both source tracks.

## Success conditions

A positive result requires an explicit structure-preserving map plus residual accounting. Shared vocabulary, shared group names, a subgroup chain, or equal vector-space dimensions are insufficient.

A negative result may be:

- no nontrivial common quotient under the declared view;
- a common quotient too weak to support the motivating bridge;
- a correspondence that fails one source track;
- a correspondence dependent on an unqualified assumption;
- an (E_8)-specific match destroyed by the obstruction audit.

All are valid outcomes.
