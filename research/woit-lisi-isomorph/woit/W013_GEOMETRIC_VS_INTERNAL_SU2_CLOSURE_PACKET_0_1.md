# W-SSC-013 Geometric-vs-Internal SU(2) Closure Packet 0.1

**Status:** CANDIDATE CLOSED_SCHEMA — COMPOSITIONAL

W04a's later source summary is reconstructed from current W134 and W009 authorities.

- SU(2)_R has a nontrivial action on Euclidean spacetime, including an explicit source witness.
- SU(2)_L acts trivially on Euclidean spacetime.
- The same SU(2)_L role acts nontrivially on the left-spinor carrier S_L.

The geometric/internal distinction is therefore represented by exact action structure:

SU(2)_R: nontrivial spacetime action
SU(2)_L: trivial spacetime action + nontrivial S_L action

This does not identify SU(2)_L with observed weak-gauge dynamics.
