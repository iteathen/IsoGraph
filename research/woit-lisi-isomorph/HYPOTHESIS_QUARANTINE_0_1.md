# Hypothesis Quarantine 0.1

## QH-001 — Clifford-product bridge
Candidate: a load-bearing common structure can be expressed through relations between (Cl(4,2)), (Cl(6)), their spinors, and a higher spin algebra.

Status: UNTESTED.

## QH-002 — block orthogonal embedding bridge
Candidate: a decomposition containing (mathfrak{so}(4,2)), (mathfrak{so}(6)), and an additional orthogonal factor inside a real (D_8)-type algebra is the relevant bridge to the (E_8) realization.

Status: UNTESTED.  
Guard: existence of a block-diagonal Lie subalgebra is not by itself evidence that either physical theory uses that decomposition in the proposed role.

Candidate: the programs share an algebraic quotient while differing mainly in whether the structure is interpreted globally/representation-theoretically or as a local gauge connection.

Status: UNTESTED.  
Guard: "mainly" is especially strong and must be decomposed into explicit source claims.

## Admission rule

A quarantined hypothesis may enter the comparison only after:

1. both source tracks are sealed independently;
2. the claimed structures are reconstructible from each sealed graph;
3. an explicit mapping preserves all load-bearing relations under the declared view;
4. residual differences are preserved;
5. applicable NEI/QU/DTS questions are resolved or carried as unknown;
6. at least one plausible falsifier has been attempted.

Failure is a research result and must be retained.
