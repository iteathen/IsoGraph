## 0. Governing objective

The research objective is the **most complete source-faithful unification that the reduced structures actually support**.

The campaign does not begin by selecting the mathematical object that ought to unify the two programs.

It begins by removing as much theory-specific packaging as lawful source fidelity permits.

The working rule is:

~~~text
source semantics
-> complete Source Semantic Census
-> primitive / exact-schema rendering
-> recursive source-local IA closure
-> NEI / DTS
-> blind structural discovery over reduced support
-> emergent correspondence classes
-> only then construct synthesis candidates
-> maximize viable reconstruction
-> minimize redundant hypotheses at the end
~~~

## 1. Discovery, not theory fitting

The following are prohibited as factorization priors:

- expected physical unification architecture;
- familiar grand-unification hierarchies;
- a preferred global carrier;
- E8 or any exceptional algebra as destination;
- triality as a required mechanism;
- twistor space as the required final carrier;
- Cartan geometry as the required global geometry;
- a direct sum such as V + S_minus + S_plus merely because it looks natural;
- current U1/U2/U3/U4 labels;
- a source-native cross-reference;
- terminology similarity;
- an analogy imported from standard physics literature.

Any of these may reappear later if the primitive structures regenerate them.

## 3. Full source conservation first

The full Woit and Lisi source tracks remain independent.

For each track:

1. complete source traversal;
2. freeze the complete Source Semantic Census;
3. conserve every load-bearing item;
4. reduce every definitionally reducible occurrence;
5. preserve genuine source unknowns as qualified QU where appropriate;
6. leave missing definitions as INCOMPLETE_UNEXPANDED rather than disguising them as QU.

No synthesis artifact may close a source-track obligation.

## 4. Reduction depth

Reduce as far as possible within the declared source semantics.

Permitted authoritative endpoints are those allowed by current Core 0.21:

- qualified Core primitive;
- primitive logical structure;
- exact raw carrier/value atom;
- exact primitive extensional incidence;
- exact all-and-only Schema Closure whose own semantics are primitive-closed;
- lawful qualified QU boundary whose known semantics are primitive-closed.

A familiar mathematical object is not an admissible stopping point merely because it is standard.

## 5. Recursive IA

IA is source-local until both tracks seal.

Each IA pass may use only:
- the current track's frozen SSC;
- its current primitive graph;
- applicable qualified mathematical authority;
- its already admitted IA support;
- its QU/NEI/DTS state under the frozen profile.

Generate consequences recursively until a no-change pass occurs for the complete fixed-point tuple.

Any load-bearing change reopens IA closure.

## 6. Transition structure

Static correspondence is insufficient when the source semantics include transformation.

DTS must expose, when load-bearing:
- preserved structure;
- removed structure;
- added structure;
- boundary/interface;
- role reassignment;
- conjugation or real-form change;
- projective chart change;
- symmetry breaking;
- representation change;
- source revision;
- unresolved transition anatomy.

Same endpoints do not establish Transition Isomorph.

## 7. Blind comparison packet

After both source tracks have sealed for the comparison scope, build a comparison packet in which source/domain gloss is removed from the structures presented to the correspondence search.

The packet should retain only load-bearing structural information such as:
- primitive relation identity/arity;
- ordered incidence;
- scope/binding;
- carrier incidence;
- multiplicity;
- exact schema structure;
- QU regions;
- transition anatomy;
- provenance handles sufficient for later reconstruction but unavailable as semantic hints to the matcher.

The matching/search stage must not know which packet came from Woit or Lisi when that information is unnecessary to structural matching.

## 8. NEI and Discovery Protocol

Run NEI on candidate natural/domain identity questions only after primitive support is available.

Run current Discovery Protocol on:
- the primitive kernels;
- source-local IA consequences;
- DTS structures;
- anonymized comparison projections.

Derived views may assist search, but every admitted discovery must project back to primitive support.

DP discrepancy is treated as evidence and a clue, not immediately as an error.

## 9. Emergent correspondence classes

The output of discovery is not immediately a unified theory.

Record:
- exact common structure;
- representation-only correspondences;
- role conflicts;
- residuals;
- QU;
- transition correspondences;
- obstructions;
- unexpected structures that cross the original theory packaging.

Only after these classes are visible may a new U candidate be proposed.

## 10. Maximal then minimal

Construction has two distinct optimization phases.

### Discovery / construction phase

Seek the largest structure that:
- reconstructs the admitted source scopes;
- preserves residual distinctions;
- survives falsifiers;
- does not resolve QU by fiat;
- uses explicit synthesis hypotheses for genuinely new relations.

### Qualification / minimization phase

After maximal viable structure is known:
- remove every synthesis edge whose deletion preserves the claimed reconstruction;
- isolate the minimum sufficient synthesis support;
- preserve larger optional extensions separately.

Thus maximal discovery and minimal qualification are complementary rather than competing goals.

U0 and existing U1 artifacts remain valid research evidence at their declared scopes.

~~~text

~~~

U1.0 may later reappear as a component of an emergent larger structure, or it may remain only a valid scoped projection.
