**Status:** APPLIED TO CURRENT RESEARCH TARGET

**New target:** corpus 0.2 / Experiment 001 successor protocol  
**Date:** 2026-10-03

## Reason

The project owner identified a load-bearing methodological requirement after the initial target was frozen:

> each author may express substantially the same idea in several different formulations, and the formulation that best exposes a cross-domain bridge may not be the formulation initially selected.

A one-rendering-per-author corpus could therefore create both false negatives and false positives.

Under Core 0.21 this is not repaired by silently changing the old corpus. It creates a new target.
