## 1. Problem and alternatives

The prior approach repeatedly examined PDF-derived SVG ink, stroke endpoints, clip geometry, and code fixtures while L01 Figure1 meaning and six-source cold reconstruction stayed open. The graphical findings and all failed and successful tests remain valid evidence **only in their stated scopes**. The following alternatives are not equivalent:

| Candidate | Advantages | Fundamental risk | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: SVG geometry first for every element | Exact graphic facts when the drawing actually matters | Renderer paths are not source claims or physical relations; open-ended micro-repairs | Not default; use only as an escalated source-fidelity tool |
| B: Original-source semantics first, then gated mechanical arbitration | Directly targets source meaning, modality, support, and omission | Initial assisted reading is not independent cold verification | **Selected as experimental L-only method** |
| C: Prose/equations alone, ignore source tables and figures | Faster first pass | Violates complete source coverage | Rejected |
| D: Prior Woit/unification bridge dictates L interpretation | Appears convenient | Cross-track contamination and circularity | Forbidden |

The design is a **change in research ordering**, not a contraction of what G0 must ultimately reconstruct. All figures, table cells, exact matrices, mathematical claims and the original six-source scope remain obligations.

## 2. Review order and evidence ownership

1. **Freeze and locate.** Pin the original author/source revision, PDF page index, source span, original source-first locus, and existing SSC owners. PDF-to-SVG is a *renderer artifact*, not an original author graph.
2. **Read without an authored candidate.** First author-side pass uses original paper only; preserve explicit expressions, source-prose semantics, modalities (asserts, proposes, conjectures, 'may'), negative statements, conditions, citations, and the figure's printed content. Record a source-first atom for each load-bearing distinguishable claim. A reader previously exposed to the candidate MUST label this pass non-blind. Do not claim independence by using a different parser.
3. **Separate carriers.** Use distinct evidence channels: source prose, printed equation, figure/caption, observation of graphic ink, and interpreter-generated hypothesis. A statement in source prose does not prove the figure draws an edge; visible ink contact does not prove field action.
4. **Source-complete coverage check.** Join every atom to the source-first inverse and existing SSC owner(s), flag missing obligations and inaccurate owners as *proposed* corrections, and preserve the existing census. Do not say a source location is complete unless its in-scope load-bearing content, figures, formulas, and footnotes have been exhausted.
5. **Explicit nondetermination.** If an authorial diagram does not specify a unique topology or physics role, preserve the distinct compatible interpretations and the evidence necessary to discriminate them. Do not claim semantic QU merely because the reviewer has not completed a required transcription or mathematical definition; missing work is incomplete.
6. **Independent image/source verification.** Issue a source-only blind packet WITHOUT source interpretation candidates, projected answers, Woit semantics, or earlier edge matrices. An independent cold reader reads the original paper/figure. Blind output is compared with the author-side inventory only after it is frozen. Mark model isolation separately from external truth authority.
7. **Reconcile discrepancies as evidence.** Record source defect vs renderer defect vs misread scope vs actual mathematical tension vs underdetermination as competing explanations. Do not patch expected outputs toward agreement. Preserve rejected interpretations and every failed test.
8. **Escalate proportionally.** Exact vector path, clip, raster, mathematical AST, or independent source reproduction is required only for a specific load-bearing disputed assertion. Do not use pixel-level agreement as a substitute for independent semantics.
9. **Close only when earned.** The unmodified full treatment contract still requires frozen full SSC, support/closure, reconstruction, IA/NEI/DTS/DP/EI and all six original-source cold reviews before sealing G0 and authorizing later gates.

## 3. Pilot benchmark and acceptance criteria

The first author-side original-source pilot is the narrow Figure1 and Section 1.1 interval on PDF page indices 2 and 3: source locations L01-S004 through L01-S009. Start with the exact first-party PDF and **do not consult the existing Figure1 interpretation when constructing the source-only inventory**. For this transitional pilot the present agent has already seen earlier work, so it is explicitly NOT a cold reviewer.

Minimum local method-preflight gates: (i) every provisional source atom declares its exact page, source location, evidence channel, modality and SSC owner; (ii) referenced source loci and existing owners match the frozen source-first mapping; (iii) no Figure1 geometric contact is promoted to an action relation; (iv) at least one unresolved case is preserved without false SAME/DISTINCT or semantic QU; (v) the candidate and the independent blind packet are separated; (vi) source counts, histories and W data remain untouched. These checks certify only internal pilot hygiene.

The first scientifically meaningful success measure is an *independent* reader's correct recovery of source meaning, with explicit coverage, cross-reader disagreements and negative controls. Counts of SVG paths, verifier versions, micro-commits, and synthetic mutants **do not** substitute for that measure. Do not infer success from a green workflow without checking that the intended fields were actually exercised.

## 4. Stop conditions and escalation

- When a new geometric calculation cannot change a specific recorded source-semantic obligation or distinguish live interpretations, **defer** it; do not erase the open obligation.
- When image-only reviewers cannot determine the intended drawn relation, retain the ambiguity and exact reasons. A source-semantic gap may be bounded as source-nondetermination without inventing a unique edge, but its SSC routing and remaining cold review still require adjudication.
- Any exact mathematical expression whose terms, coefficient, root domain or sign remain unread stays INCOMPLETE_UNEXPANDED until reconstructed.
- Qualification, source correctness, proposed physics, empirical adequacy, and cross-author unification are separate dispositions.
- The present protocol cannot authorize G1, rewrite SSC 0.96, seal any of six sources, merge PR #70, change qualified Core, or import Woit semantics.
