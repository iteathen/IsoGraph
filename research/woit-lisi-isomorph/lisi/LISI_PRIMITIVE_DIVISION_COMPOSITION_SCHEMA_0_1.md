## 216003

216003 starts from the already rendered composition-algebra schema 185003 and expands the source term **division algebra** without assuming associativity.

For every nonzero (a) and every (b) in the algebra carrier:

~~~text
a x = b
~~~

has exactly one carrier solution (x), and

~~~text
y a = b
~~~

has exactly one carrier solution (y).

This is strong enough for ordinary octonionic division semantics and does not reduce division to an associative inverse law.

The schema says nothing about topology, completeness, finite dimension, or a named algebra. Source instances separately supply those structures.
