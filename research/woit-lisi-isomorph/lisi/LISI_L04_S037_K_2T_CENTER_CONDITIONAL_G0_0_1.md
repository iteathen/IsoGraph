## Source premises and conditional deduction

The author writes that K commutes with P,T,t but anticommutes with C; that P,T generate Q8 and adjoining t yields binary tetrahedral 2T, **including K**; and that 2T shares the center {+1,-1} with the other named normal subgroups.

If **all these refer to the same faithful, unquotiented operator carrier**, let H=<P,T,t> be exactly 2T with center {+1,-1}. K belongs to H and commutes with all its generators, hence K belongs to Z(H)={+1,-1}. Either real scalar +1 or -1 commutes with an invertible operator C, including when C is antilinear. That cannot simultaneously satisfy KC=-CK in characteristic zero. Thus the literal joint assumptions are inconsistent. **This does not determine which source role, quotient or representation distinction is responsible.**

Exact rational Hamilton-quaternion closure of <e1,e2,-(1+e1+e2+e3)/2> locally returns 24 elements with center {+1,-1}. A separately typed complex-coefficient/right-quaternion semilinear model returns <P,T> order8, <C,P,T> order16, <P,T,t> order48 and <C,P,T,t> order96, and K belongs to the latter subgroup while commuting with P/T/t and anticommuting with C. Those model results were checked within the bounded [34-hostile S036 CI](https://github.com/iteathen/IsoGraph/actions/runs/38208109739); they **do not qualify** source 2T membership, its GAP identity or physical realization.

## Competing explanations and falsifiers

1. **Carrier switch:** quaternionic 2T and the complex-antilinear K operator live in distinct faithful representations. Require an explicit source-licensed carrier map and inverse.
2. **Scoped quotient/central extension:** a 48-element operator closure may project to a 24-element object, but verify the kernel and whether the C anticommutation descends. A global sign quotient cannot simply erase its distinguishing phase without checking C.
3. **Overloaded K notation:** the group K and creation-conjugation K might differ by a representational convention. Require original-scoped typed provenance rather than a guessed identification.
4. **Literal author mathematics error:** retain as a falsifiable alternative, **not an established defect**, until other explanations are independently excluded.
