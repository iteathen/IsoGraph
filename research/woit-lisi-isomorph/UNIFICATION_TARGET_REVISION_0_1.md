## New primary question

Given independent source-faithful renderings W and L and a demonstrated shared kernel K, construct the smallest useful candidate U such that:

1. the selected Woit structure reconstructs inside U;
2. the selected Lisi structure reconstructs inside U;
3. both reconstructions agree on K;
4. no source-specific residual is silently identified with an unrelated residual;
5. contradictions create explicit branches or QU, not forced collapse;
6. genuinely new relations introduced by the synthesis are labeled as project-generated hypotheses.

The intended object is a **minimal conservative amalgam**, not a union of terminology.

## Historical common kernel

The frozen BT01 algebraic/projective projection is the initial shared kernel K.

It contains, at the currently demonstrated scope:

- quaternionic carrier structure;
- two chiral roles and the Spin(4)-type action skeleton;
- typed cross-chiral action;
- sign-correct chiral Cl(0,4) coefficient structure;
- real/complex representation transport;
- pseudoreal/projective structure;
- graph/projectivization;
- affine quaternionic twistor incidence.

It does not contain by default:

- Woit's full global physical interpretation;
- Lisi's triality, magic-square, or E8 structure;
- Woit's or Lisi's full dynamics;
- an assertion that the two programs are physically equivalent.

## Synthesis firewall

Unification artifacts are project-generated.

They may consume frozen source renderings, qualified mathematical support, and frozen common-kernel evidence.

They may not rewrite what Woit or Lisi asserted, close a missing source obligation, turn a project-generated relation into an author claim, or unquarantine a source-native cross-reference by convenience.

Exploratory U-candidates may be constructed before the full tracks seal.

Promotion to a qualified unification result requires both source tracks sealed, post-seal NEI/DP/DTS comparison, reconstruction in both directions for the claimed scope, falsifiers, and relevant obstruction audits.
