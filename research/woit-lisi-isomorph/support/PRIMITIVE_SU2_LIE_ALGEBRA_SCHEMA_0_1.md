# Primitive su(2)-Type Lie Algebra Presentation 0.1

**Status:** RESEARCH-LOCAL LIE-ALGEBRA SUPPORT  
**Native:** PRIMITIVE_SU2_LIE_ALGEBRA_SCHEMA_0_1.isg

207001 gives an exact three-dimensional Lie-algebra presentation with basis t1,t2,t3 and commutator normalization:

~~~text
[t1,t2] = 2 t3
[t2,t3] = 2 t1
[t3,t1] = 2 t2.
~~~

The full bilinear, antisymmetric, Jacobi semantics come from 184004.

The scalar 2 is required to be nonzero.

This is the algebraic presentation naturally realized by the imaginary quaternion / anti-Hermitian Pauli basis. It does not assert topology or a global SU(2) group.
