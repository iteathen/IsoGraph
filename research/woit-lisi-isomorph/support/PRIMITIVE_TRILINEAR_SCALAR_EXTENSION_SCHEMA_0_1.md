# Primitive Trilinear Scalar-Extension Schema 0.1

**Status:** RESEARCH-LOCAL GENERIC SUPPORT  
**Native:** `PRIMITIVE_TRILINEAR_SCALAR_EXTENSION_SCHEMA_0_1.isg`

## 225000

225000 represents a scalar extension of a real trilinear form to an explicitly represented larger field.

It requires:

- a field embedding `R -> C`;
- three real vector spaces and three target `C`-vector spaces;
- restriction of the target scalar actions back to `R`;
- injective real-linear maps from each real role into its target;
- a scalar trilinear form `T_R` on the real roles;
- a scalar trilinear form `T_C` on the target roles;
- exact agreement of `T_C` with the field-embedded value of `T_R` on every embedded real triple.

The schema does not itself assert that the target spaces are generated by the embedded real spaces. Source instances that need a genuine complexification must separately provide scalar-extension/basis closure, such as schemas 222000–222002.

For L05, this is the support needed to evaluate generalized-reflection outputs carrying explicit `sqrt(s_u)=i` phases without identifying those outputs with the original real roles.
