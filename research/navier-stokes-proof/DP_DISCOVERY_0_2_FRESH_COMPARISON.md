## Strongest combined architectural hypothesis

The current evidence suggests a layered mechanism:

1. **Eliminate constraints structurally**
   - divergence freedom by representation;
   - unwanted nonlinear cross-coupling by support coloring.

2. **Normalize the remaining defect**
   - exact labelwise/mean/error residual decomposition.

3. **Repair by typed channels**
   - particular, signed, temporal, rank.

4. **Remove finite obstruction**
   - FiveRows/debt interface.

5. **Increase approximation order**
   - common invariant frame + sigma recurrence.

6. **Extract a smooth limit**
   - diagonal schedule.

7. **Close the PDE contract**
   - smooth residual-derived force, support, energy.

8. **Forget internals**
   - transport and uniqueness consume only candidate contracts.

This combined hypothesis is not yet a qualified minimal-generator theorem.
