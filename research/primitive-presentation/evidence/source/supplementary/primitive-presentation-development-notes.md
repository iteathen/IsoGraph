These are working notes, not part of the formal manuscript and not a source to cite. They preserve earlier vocabulary, discarded framings, motivation, examples, and future directions. The current manuscript does not use the older labels as its formal terminology; it uses the schematic role symbols α, β, and γ under fair accounting.

Read these notes as a development record, not as a cleaned position. Some entries use exploratory, metaphorical, or overstrong language because they were written while the idea was still being found. Where the notes sound like physics, ontology, empirical law, or a settled external mapping, the current interpretation is weaker: those passages record candidate intuitions and test prompts, not claims the paper is entitled to assert.

They also preserve the human path into the argument. The main manuscript is intentionally formal, but the idea did not begin as formal polish. It began as a pressure around honest accounting: if a symbolic system does work, then every dependency that makes the work possible should be counted, whether it appears as a glyph, a parser, an evaluator, a convention, a rule, a state discipline, or a readout.

These notes should remain useful but non-load-bearing:

```text
use them to remember motivation, discarded framings, examples, and future directions;
do not use them as premises for the proof;
```

## Historical Core Position

The older framing treated the role triad too much like a theory-level object. The current manuscript replaces that with dependency-question language:

```text
α: what carries effective transition or advance?
β: what supplies determinate presence?
γ: what settles how advance bears on presence?
```

The visible delimiter syntax is only a witness syntax. SKI is a canonical witness and comparison point. Dynamic execution sketches are examples of counted apparatus, not premises of the theorem.

## Discussion Comment Summary

The cleaned discussion contains a long exploratory thread about using AI as a
search tool for hidden structural relations across mathematics and physics. The
important comments to preserve are not individual prompts, but the recurring
constraints they imposed on the project.

Core motivation:

```text
Use AI to search for symmetries, intersections, isomorphisms, and translations
between mathematical and physical structures that may not have been compared
directly before.
```

The project began from the suspicion that many apparent primitives may actually
be derivatives of a deeper productive structure. If physical and mathematical
complexity can be generated from a very small primitive basis, then the
important task is not to name the visible symbols but to find the roles and
dependencies that make generation possible.

Important recurring comments:

```text
Do not assume the visible symbol count is the real primitive count.

Do not treat familiar constants, dimensions, or equations as safe validation
targets until their hidden assumptions are understood.

Do not use visual resemblance as evidence unless it supports prediction,
decoding, compression, or formal preservation.

Do not build restricted graph boundaries into the fundamental logic. Resolution
can vary locally, but the underlying system should not depend on an artificial
finite wall.

Do not treat a collapse-looking region as terminal collapse without checking
for local minima, delayed recovery, negative continuation, cycles, or other
longer-run behavior.

Do not treat disconnected regions as automatically invalid. A disconnected
component may be outside the observed reality while still being a valid branch
of the generative system.

Do not constrain the model to three or four dimensions as a validity filter.
Dimensional behavior can be measured, but it should not be assumed in advance.

Do not settle for absolute truth as the goal. The serious goal is accumulating
machine-checkable confidence while keeping the remaining uncertainty explicit.
```

This discussion also sharpened the role of isomorphism. A mapping should not be
understood as a simple label replacement. A serious mapping has to preserve the
relevant behavior of one system inside another system:

```text
translation is not enough;
alignment is not enough;
the mapping has to preserve operations, relations, predictions, or compression.
```

The validation instinct was adversarial from the start. The useful question was
not "can the system produce something interesting?" but:

```text
can it fail honestly;
can it detect when it is fooling itself;
can it distinguish a primitive from a derivative;
can it expose where the evaluator, parser, boundary, convention, or readout is
doing hidden work;
can it find early witness tests before a year is spent training toward an
unreachable high-level target?
```

Physical constants were discussed as possible witness targets, but with strong
caution. Constants should be treated relationally or dimensionlessly where
possible, not as unit-bound numbers. High-level constants such as the speed of
light may depend on too much already-formed structure to be good early tests.
More primitive behavioral signals may be better:

```text
causal invariance
path-connectedness
stable growth
persistent local structure
cycle formation
interaction of regions
compression over time
predictive recovery of held-out behavior
```

The later three-role dynamic sketch grew out of this same pressure. The fixed audit
model was explored with the relation:

```text
p / z = 2 +/- QF
```

The intended philosophical reading was:

```text
the code supplies the rule and the noise;
the resulting structure should not be hardcoded;
if anything stable appears, it should appear because it satisfies the audit.
```

The discussion around noise density and retry loops matters because it marks a
shift from a theatrical demonstration to an honest test. Too much noise makes
emergence mechanically inevitable. Too little noise can leave the system empty.
A retry or recursive pulse mechanism tests whether the rule can repeatedly
search a near-empty vacuum until a stable breakout appears without planting a
specific seed.

The multi-point ignition observation should be remembered carefully:

```text
two initial active regions are not necessarily a bug;
they may show that the rule accepts any local point that satisfies the audit;
later collision or interference between regions is a separate phenomenon to
measure, not a reason to pretend there was only one origin.
```

Naming pressure from the discussion:

```text
the current dependency-question vocabulary is preferred for the formal role abstraction.
law-style language may be useful in philosophical or dynamic sketches, but it
risks sounding like an empirical physics claim unless carefully bounded.
```

The strongest paper-relevant lesson from the discussion is this:

```text
The project is about fair accounting of productive dependencies. If a system
appears to use fewer primitives, the missing work must be carried somewhere:
in roles, rules, state, parser discipline, application structure, boundary
conditions, evaluator behavior, or readout.
```

Keep this summary outside the proof. It is useful because it records the
author's pressure toward skepticism, validation, and honest accounting. It is
not itself evidence for the theorem.

These are ideas that helped guide the reasoning but should not be allowed to
clutter the proof spine unless they are later made precise.


## Fixed-Audit Dynamic Sketch

One useful developmental model treats a field or graph as a configuration updated by a fixed audit:

```text
active iff p == 2z
```

Useful discipline:

```text
the rule is fixed;
local conditions or perturbations vary;
structure counts only if it persists reproducibly;
similarity is not evidence unless it supports prediction, decoding, or compression.
```

This is potentially useful as an example of the dependency-question framework in a dynamic setting:

```text
β: current field, graph, row, history, saved state, or readable output
α: local update, perturbation-to-transition, rewrite, or evolution step
γ: audit, update order, boundary policy, readout convention, survival test
```

Keep this outside the proof unless the model is used only to illustrate fair accounting.

## Isomorphism Testing

A useful standard for future claims:

```text
encode: source state or structure -> target representation
evolve: target representation predicts future behavior
decode: target representation -> source observables
preserve: relevant operations, relations, or ratios are preserved
transfer: the mapping works across multiple seeds/runs/examples
```

Visual resemblance or a single successful run is not enough. A mapping becomes serious only when it predicts held-out behavior or compresses behavior better than a baseline.

## Naming Strategy

I do not want the older labels to look like a new naming scheme pasted on top of
preexisting systems for its own sake. The deeper preference is to broaden how
existing systems are understood: SKI and related minimal systems should be read
as productive apparatuses with roles, not merely as narrow named symbol sets.

The practical problem is that asking everyone to redefine an existing system
with a broader scope is harder than introducing a carefully bounded vocabulary.
the dependency-question framework gives me terms I can define cleanly and use consistently without first
winning a fight over how SKI, binary calculi, automata, or other established
systems ought to be reframed.

Preferred philosophical direction:

```text
If possible, expand the interpretation of existing systems to include their
full productive apparatus and role structure.
```

Pragmatic manuscript direction:

```text
Use the dependency-question framework as a new vocabulary for the role abstraction, and treat existing
systems as witnesses or realizations of that abstraction.
```

This is not meant to replace the historical systems. It is a way to make the
role-accounting point without depending on broad agreement about how those
systems should be redescribed.
