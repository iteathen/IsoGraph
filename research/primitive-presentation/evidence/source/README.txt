Scope note:
The active paper is primitive-presentation.md. Its current terminology is the
three dependency questions named by α, β, and γ under fair accounting. The
claim is not that every system has three separate components, but that every
productive effective presentation must answer those dependency questions
somewhere in its counted apparatus.

are not premises of the proof and should not be treated as established
