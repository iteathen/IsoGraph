## Authority boundary

Publication status, peer/agent review, and research interest do not by themselves promote a claim into IsoGraph semantic authority. Current family routing remains controlled by exact versioned specifications and qualification records.

When a publication has a corresponding research project, its project README should remain the primary consolidated research dossier and should link the publication plus the supporting evidence.
