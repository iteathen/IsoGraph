# Anonymous case 03B semantic sidecar

Two scalar coordinates q(t), p(t) over a continuous parameter t, with positive constants m and k.

Define a scalar generator:
```text
H(q,p)=p^2/(2m)+kq^2/2
```

and governing first-order relations:
```text
dq/dt = p/m
dp/dt = -kq
```

No second-order governing equation is supplied.
