# Anonymous case 01A semantic sidecar

Finite indexed sites with local value x_i in {-1,+1}. A fixed symmetric neighbor relation selects interacting pairs.

State score:
```text
E_A(x) = -a * sum_<ij> x_i x_j - b * sum_i x_i
```

With inverse-scale parameter t:
```text
w_A(x)=exp(-t E_A(x))
Z_A=sum_x w_A(x)
P_A(x)=w_A(x)/Z_A
```

The comparison may alpha-map identifiers but may not treat this prose label or file position as structural evidence.
