## Purpose

IsoGraph is primarily a specification- and research-driven project. This repository contains versioned specifications, research records, publications, evidence, reference tooling, source code, data, diagrams, and historical artifacts.

This notice makes four boundaries explicit:

1. what copyright is being claimed in the repository's original expression;
2. what the existing repository license does and does not cover;
3. the distinction between the IsoGraph method and the documents that describe it;
4. the distinction between implementing IsoGraph and representing an implementation as official, certified, or endorsed by the IsoGraph Project.

This notice is intended to clarify the existing repository regime. It does **not** retroactively rewrite frozen historical artifacts and does **not** add restrictions to rights already granted under the repository's AGPL-3.0 license.

## 2. Method / expression boundary

IsoGraph describes procedures, systems, methods, structural representations, inference rules, research processes, and mathematical or logical constructions.

Copyright protection in the repository's documents applies to copyrightable **expression**. It does not create a copyright monopoly over an uncopyrightable idea, procedure, process, system, method of operation, concept, principle, discovery, fact, or mathematical relationship merely because that subject is described here.

Accordingly:

- copying, modifying, or redistributing copyrightable repository expression is governed by the applicable repository/file license;
- independently implementing a method described by IsoGraph is a separate question and is not converted into copying of the specification merely by implementing the method;
- an independent implementation does not thereby become an official IsoGraph release, a qualified IsoGraph artifact, or an endorsed IsoGraph service.

This distinction follows the ordinary U.S. copyright boundary reflected in 17 U.S.C. § 102(b) and U.S. Copyright Office Circular 33, *Works Not Protected by Copyright*.

## 5. Official and modified specifications

The official IsoGraph specification lineage is the versioned lineage maintained by the IsoGraph Project and routed by its current authority/qualification records.

A modified specification or fork should:

- identify that it has been modified;
- preserve applicable copyright and license notices;
- preserve factual upstream provenance;
- avoid presenting modified semantics as an unchanged official IsoGraph qualification;
- identify the upstream revision from which it was derived when that revision is material.

Frozen historical evidence should not be rewritten merely to insert newer rights language. Current routing and explanatory documents should link to this notice instead.

## 6. Provenance and misrepresentation boundary

IsoGraph's public provenance records intentionally distinguish:

- project direction;
- exact formulation;
- implementation;
- research finding;
- agent assistance;
- external antecedents;
- publication authorship.

The project does not claim that every formulation or finding was solely originated by one contributor.

At the same time, republication, modification, or commentary should not knowingly erase or falsify factual provenance. Where AGPL-3.0 requires preservation of notices or prominent notice of modification, those license obligations control.

Nothing here turns an evidence-scoped project chronology into a claim of worldwide priority.
