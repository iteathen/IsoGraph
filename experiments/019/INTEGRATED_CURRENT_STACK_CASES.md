# Experiment 019 — Fresh Integrated Current-Stack Holdout

## I01 — Shared unknown carrier versus constrained-equal unknown carriers

System A represents A.x and B.x as references to one shared QU carrier u.

System B represents A.x -> u1 and B.x -> u2 with distinct SIs plus an exact value-equality constraint value(u1)=value(u2).

Determine whether these are the same QU information state and whether natural identity of A and B follows.

## I02 — QU-conditioned probabilistic identity evidence

For every QU realization r, an observation E has a defined log Bayes factor w(E|r), but the value varies across realizations. QU supplies no probability distribution over realizations.

Determine whether NEI may silently average to one scalar evidence weight and what must be preserved instead.

## I03 — Exact identity theorem plus probabilistic evidence

A qualified exact domain theorem forces a and b coidentical in every admissible identity model. Separate empirical evidence has finite positive log Bayes factor.

Determine the exact NEI status and whether the finite evidence weight is what makes the status exact.

## I04 — Structural discovery without identity closure

DP establishes a deep structural correspondence between A and B. A natural-identity question is then asked, but the required identity model/closure authority is absent.

Determine whether DP may answer SAME and whether NEI returns semantic UNKNOWN or an incomplete/unqualified identity query.

## I05 — Different semantic quantities inside one qualified representation

A frozen representation is complete for its declared evidence corpus. One report says 18 citation occurrences; another says 6 artifacts. Both definitions and values are correct.

Determine whether the representation must fail qualification merely because the values differ and whether the discrepancy can still expose a hidden-distinction discovery lead.

## I06 — Real decoder error plus surviving discovery structure

A decoder omits one explicit relation; exact source inspection proves the decoder defect. The omitted relation is repeatedly derivable from two retained relations across independent examples.

Determine whether the decoder error can be repaired while preserving an open structural lead.

## I07 — Scoped quotient plus unresolved natural identity

Under frozen comparison view C, structures A and B are exactly equivalent after projecting distinction D, whose irrelevance to C is qualified. For the broader natural-identity question, admissible identity models include both coidentity and separation.

Determine the scoped structural conclusion and the exact NEI result.

## I08 — Partial common core with residual

Two independently represented workflows have an exact common structural core over roles R1-R4. The target has an additional load-bearing relation R5 that is outside the mapped common core.

Determine whether DP may claim full/global isomorphism and whether R5 must remain a residual.

## I09 — Correlated identity evidence

Three evidence records originate from one measurement event and common causal lineage. Each record individually has log Bayes factor +2 under the same identity hypothesis.

No conditional-independence authority exists.

Determine whether NEI may sum them to +6 as independent evidence.

## I10 — QU refinement changes exact identity result

At revision Q1, the qualified admissible identity-model family contains SAME and DISTINCT models, so NEI is UNKNOWN. A later genuine QU refinement Q2 excludes all SAME-compatible realizations and leaves a nonempty family where every model separates the subjects.

Determine the Q2 exact result and whether the historical Q1 UNKNOWN record is rewritten.

## I11 — Classification difference versus natural distinctness

Two represented subjects have different application class labels but no domain law makes that classification identity-separating.

Determine whether NEI may infer DISTINCT from the class labels alone.

## I12 — Identity-driven QU restriction is circular

An investigator wants SAME, removes every DISTINCT-compatible QU realization solely for that reason, and then uses the restricted QU as evidence that SAME is exact.

Determine whether this is admissible.

## I13 — Qualification completion with open discovery lead

A representation satisfies every declared qualification obligation. A preserved discrepancy remains an open DP lead, but it does not undermine the representation's declared completeness/accuracy claim.

Determine whether qualification may complete and whether the discovery lead must close.

## I14 — Falsified anomaly

A surprising mismatch is preserved, quantities/scopes are aligned, plausible hidden-structure explanations are tested and falsified, and a local implementation error is proved.

Determine whether DP may terminate the structural lead without inventing an equivalence.
