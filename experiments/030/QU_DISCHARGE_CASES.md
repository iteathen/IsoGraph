# Experiment 030 — Fresh QU-Invariant Support Cases

## R01 — Refined QU, invariant conclusion

Qualified QU state Q0 admits {a,b,c}.

Independently qualified evidence strictly refines it to Q1={a,b}.

Pinned authority proves proposition P in every realization of Q1.

The source does not explicitly state P.

Determine whether P may receive exact implicit support, whether the assertion body itself may remain the plain proposition P, whether Q1 remains part of the support/scope dependency, and whether provenance must retain Q0->Q1 plus the universality witness.

## R02 — Invariant across the original QU family

Qualified QU state Q admits four admissible realizations.

Pinned authority proves proposition K in every realization of Q.

Nothing else is needed to state the proposition K.

Determine whether K may receive exact implicit support, whether the assertion body may be K without embedding an explicit QU conditional operator, whether the QU dependency may disappear from provenance, and whether this establishes which actual realization occurred.

## R03 — Admissible realizations disagree

Qualified QU state Q admits r1 and r2.

Pinned authority proves:

- P in r1;
- not P in r2.

No refinement is available.

Determine whether P may receive unconditional exact implicit support, whether QU may be discharged from the conclusion, and whether the unresolved possibility structure remains load-bearing.

## R04 — Scope escape after invariant support

Q0 is refined to Q1.

P is proven invariant across every realization of Q1 and therefore receives exact implicit support under that support scope.

A later consumer strips all reference to the Q1 support/scope and uses P as if it had been established under the broader Q0 state.

No theorem proves P across all Q0 realizations.

Determine whether the later use is valid, whether Q1 support scope must remain attached to the implicit assertion/support record, and whether conclusion/body discharge authorizes scope escape.

## R05 — Same body, different support lineages

One exact implicit support for assertion body P is established under QU refinement Q0->Q1.

A second independent exact implicit support for the same body P is established under different QU refinement Z0->Z1.

Both are valid under their own scopes and witnesses.

Determine whether the assertion body may be shared, whether the two support lineages become identical merely because the body matches, and whether both QU/refinement provenances remain recoverable.

## R06 — Semantic guard is not QU uncertainty

Under explicit guard G, every admissible realization of qualified QU Q satisfies P.

Outside G, authority does not establish P.

A reasoner proves universality across Q while G holds.

Determine whether QU may be discharged from the conclusion/body through invariance, whether guard G may also be dropped, whether exact support remains conditional on G, and whether the QU universality provenance remains recoverable.
