# Experiment 054 — DP 0.10 Experimental Warrant holdout

## W01 — Already closed
A finite represented proof discharges the active claim under the complete declared authority. The proposed experiment would remeasure the same already proved relation.

## W02 — Coherent unresolved residual
Three independently derived structures leave the same unexplained residual interface. Existing represented reasoning cannot decide whether it is a shared constraint or two different mechanisms. A new observation at that interface would distinguish the live alternatives.

## W03 — QU-shaped hole
A qualified QU has two admissible coupled realizations. Their interface topology identifies one boundary where a new measurement would refine the family, but nothing chooses either realization in advance.

## W04 — Detailed QU plus possible missing dimension
A QU precisely represents all currently characterized uncertainty, but repeated observations have produced a material residual not representable by any current QU dimension. No completeness theorem says the possibility model is exhaustive.

## W05 — Unknown experimental dimensionality
Evidence implicates a response boundary but does not identify all variables that govern it. Several candidate dimensions are named and the warrant record explicitly leaves the scope frontier open.

## W06 — Supported narrow intervention
A conservation law proves that all but one independently controlled variable are irrelevant to the active residual. Varying the remaining variable alone can distinguish the two live explanations.

## W07 — Interaction clue
All evidence for a phenomenon occurs only when parity, geometry, and controller role coincide. None of the three has independent evidence of sufficiency.

## W08 — Deferred variable
A variable remains plausibly relevant but is too costly for the first experiment. It is omitted from that experiment without evidence of irrelevance.

## W09 — Missing dependency
Two known components should connect under the target behavior, but no represented relation closes the path. Testing whether an additional coupling variable exists would determine whether the missing role is real.

## W10 — Model-breaking observation
A prior experiment returns a reproducible fourth output category while the working model represents only three. Instrument integrity checks pass.

## W11 — Narrow negative probe
A one-dimensional intervention falsifies candidate H1. The parent clue also includes an untested interaction H2 involving another dimension.

## W12 — Structural boundary
Two trajectories have different literal lengths but each reaches the same represented transition boundary where an obligation is discharged. DTS supplies the boundary anatomy.

## W13 — Alignment found after outcomes
After viewing outcomes, an investigator notices a boundary alignment that makes two curves coincide. No independent data has yet tested that selected alignment.

## W14 — Adaptive variable selection
Observation batch A is used to invent variable z and choose its threshold. The investigator then proposes citing the same batch A as independent confirmation of z.

## W15 — Parameter-grid temptation
A system has 40 tunable parameters but no material residual, clue, gap, or unresolved objective connected to them. Compute is available for a grid search.

## W16 — Warrant versus belief
A structured unexplained residual and discriminating observation class justify an experiment, but neither live explanation has supporting evidence beyond being compatible with current facts.

## W17 — Identity-sensitive clue
Two carriers may be the same natural object or distinct objects. A new observation could discriminate the admissible identity models, but current evidence does not settle identity.

## W18 — No leverage
A material unknown remains, but every feasible proposed observation is invariant across all currently admissible explanations and cannot refine scope, constraints, or consequences.
