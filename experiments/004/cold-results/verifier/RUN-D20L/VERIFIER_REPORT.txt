## 3. Detailed Failure Analysis

While the cold agent's core topological analysis, retrieval evaluation, degree-profile calculations, isomorphism mapping, and separating invariant logic are **fully correct**, its attempt to satisfy **Gate E1NR (Native Record Authority)** and Section 29 introduces a **parser/serialization defect**:

1. **Grammar Violation (`label := ^integer`)**: Section 5 of the Core Specification explicitly defines the surface grammar production:
   ```text
   label := ^integer
   ```
   Throughout the specification and native fixtures, stable labels are strictly numeric integers (e.g., `^0`, `^6005`, `^6000`). 
2. **Fabricated Symbolic Tags**: In Part V, the frozen report introduces symbolic strings preceded by carets (e.g., `^comparison_policy`, `^target_layer`, `^V0`, `^ISOMORPHISM`, `^witness`, `^verified`, etc.). Under the deterministic grammar of Section 5, these are syntactically invalid and fail mechanical parsing as native IsoGraph terms.
