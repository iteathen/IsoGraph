## Q004-11 Evaluation Report

### 1. Isomorphism Evaluation (V0)
* **Result:** `Rejected / False`
* **Justification:** Under the V0 closed structural view, stable labels are rigid. `^5001` contains exactly one member occurrence (ordered incidence): `(^5100 10 11)`. `^5002` contains two member occurrences: `(^5100 20 21)` and `(^5101 20 21)`. Because a structural isomorphism requires a bijection between member occurrences and `^5002` has a larger multiset cardinality of member occurrences, no bijective edge mapping exists. 

### 2. Strong/Induced Embedding Evaluation (V0)
* **Result:** `Rejected / False`
* **Justification:** Under Section 25 of the Core Specification, a strong embedding requires injective preservation *and reflection* of every included relation/scope/constraint kind among mapped objects. 
Let the candidate node mapping be $m: \{10, 11\} \to \{20, 21\}$ defined by $m(10) = 20$ and $m(11) = 21$. This mapping preserves the edge `(^5100 10 11)` since `(^5100 20 21)` is present in the target. However, the target contains an additional relation, `(^5101 20 21)`, on the mapped objects $\{20, 21\}$. Since the pre-image `(^5101 10 11)` is absent in the source, this relation is not reflected from the source. Because `^5101` is part of the frozen included relation signature of this run, its non-reflection among the mapped objects violates the strong embedding condition. Any other node bijection fails preservation entirely.

### 3. Weak Embedding Evaluation (V0)
* **Result:** `Verified / True`
* **Justification:** A weak embedding requires injective preservation of source structure while any extra target relations remain as explicit residuals. Under the mapping `{10 -> 20, 11 -> 21}`, the source edge `(^5100 10 11)` is preserved as `(^5100 20 21)` in the target, and the extra target-side edge `(^5101 20 21)` is successfully accounted for as a target-only pair residual.

### 4. Valid Mapping
* **Relation Kind:** `WEAK_EMBEDDING`
* **Node Correspondence:** 
  ```text
  10 -> 20
  11 -> 21
  ```
* **Edge Mapping:** 
  ```text
  (^5100 10 11) -> (^5100 20 21)
  ```

### 5. Pairwise Item Accounting & Blocking Relations
* **Mapped Common Structure:**
  * Nodes: `10` (mapped to `20`), `11` (mapped to `21`)
  * Edge: `(^5100 10 11)` (mapped to `(^5100 20 21)`)
* **Target-Only Pair Residual (B-only residual):**
  * Edge: `(^5101 20 21)`
* **Boundary-Cut / Incidence:**
  * The residual edge `(^5101 20 21)` is incident on the mapped common core nodes `20` and `21`.
* **Blocking Relation:** The target-only relation `(^5101 20 21)` holds among mapped objects but has no pre-image in the source, thereby blocking a `STRONG_EMBEDDING` and forcing a `WEAK_EMBEDDING`.
* **Ambiguity:** None. The rigid label `^5100` uniquely determines the mapping direction, preventing any alternate node permutation.
