# Experiment 059 — Core 0.21 Expanded-Family Integration Cases

## I01 — Discovery cannot shrink the rendering
A source-semantic census contains a hard-to-reduce item H. A DP search view works better when H is omitted. No scope revision exists.

## I02 — Scope revision and old results
A qualified rendering target T0 over scope S0 is revised to target T1 over narrower scope S1 with a proper SCOPE_REVISION. Earlier DP and IA results were derived under S0.

## I03 — Iterative schema with transition anatomy
An iterative system has primitive-closed initial/continuation/step semantics and exact all-and-only schema coverage. The claim also depends on the order in which information is introduced and removed at each transition.

## I04 — Recursive semantics with unresolved termination
A recursive generator is schema-closed. Whether every admissible input terminates remains a structured unresolved property represented by qualified QU.

## I05 — Deeper reduction exposes an IA
A primitive-kernel expansion changes K0 to K1 and exposes exact support for a new implicit assertion. A prior IA fixed point was recorded under K0. DP wants to use the new assertion in discovery.

## I06 — Identity claim with opaque assertion body
Primitive premises are present, but the body of a relation used to support a natural-identity conclusion still hides a definitionally reducible operator.

## I07 — Missing QU semantics
A renderer cites a qualified QU object, but one known load-bearing QU membership rule is absent from primitive support. The unresolved realizations themselves are properly preserved.

## I08 — Experimental negative result
EI produces two scoped observations that form a counterexample to a proposed universal relation over the tested declared domain. DP records the tested coverage.

## I09 — Partial materialization
A family with ten billion possible generated instances has exact primitive-closed generator semantics and all-and-only schema coverage. Only 100 instances have been materialized. A DP valuation profile prefers not to enumerate more.

## I10 — Derived search view
A derived abstraction significantly improves DP search. Deleting it leaves the exact primitive kernel and source reconstruction intact.

## I11 — Model-breaking new observation
EI returns a reproducible observation outside the current experimental working model and outside the frozen source material of an already qualified rendering campaign.

## I12 — External simulator observation
A versioned simulator produces raw observations under explicit assumptions for an EI experiment. Its revision and assumptions are recorded.

## I13 — Independent qualification gates
A candidate rendering covers every frozen census item but one item uses an invalid inference. Another candidate is sound for every included item but omits one census item.

## I14 — Silent scope drift with downstream results
A rendering's actual scope changes without SCOPE_REVISION after NEI and DP conclusions were derived under the old frozen scope.

## I15 — Ledger checker role
The deterministic Core 0.21 ledger checker verifies census coverage, structural closure, scope integrity, and fixed-point identity. A proposal treats its PASS result as theorem authority for a domain claim.

## I16 — Full authority boundary
One current-family case simultaneously uses Core 0.21 rendering conservation, a QU realization family, DTS transition anatomy, an NEI identity question, a DP Experimental Warrant, and EI observations.
