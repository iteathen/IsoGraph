# Experiment 055 — Experimental Inquiry 0.1 holdout

## Q01 — Missing warrant
A team has an interesting dataset but DP has issued NO_WARRANT: no material unresolved discovery demand and no observation-leverage rationale. They propose an exploratory experiment anyway.

## Q02 — Partial scope
A warrant identifies a recurrent boundary and two candidate variables, while explicitly stating that other dimensions may be missing. The experiment must decide what else to inspect from residual structure.

## Q03 — Proven isolation
A governing conservation relation proves all but variable x irrelevant to the target residual over the declared scope. The warrant identifies x as the only live intervention.

## Q04 — Interaction required
A warrant records that the phenomenon appears only under the joint presence of x and y and that neither has independent support alone.

## Q05 — Unfilled role
The working model has two anchored components whose required interaction lacks any represented carrier. A candidate coupling dimension can be probed and its rationale is the failed closure.

## Q06 — QU experiment
A QU preserves two coupled admissible realizations. Their interface suggests a discriminating measurement; choosing one realization beforehand would simplify the experiment.

## Q07 — Unknown unknown reserve
All current QU regions are detailed, but no completeness authority establishes that the working dimensions exhaust relevant structure.

## Q08 — Expected and unexpected output
The model predicts amplitude and phase. The instrument can also report topology changes that are not part of the current expected-output vocabulary.

## Q09 — Fourth category
A run produces a reproducible fourth structural category. Raw traces and instrument checks are available; the working model has only three categories.

## Q10 — Model break
The new category cannot be represented by any current dimension or QU constraint. The experiment mechanism itself passes its controls.

## Q11 — Adaptive threshold
Batch A is used to discover variable z and choose threshold 7. The next write-up proposes treating batch A as independent confirmation of z>7.

## Q12 — Post-hoc boundary
A useful boundary is noticed only after inspecting outcome curves. The project wants to make that boundary load-bearing for a general claim.

## Q13 — Structural-event alignment
Two trajectories have different numbers of steps but DTS shows each crosses the same interface after discharging the same represented obligation.

## Q14 — Negative narrow experiment
An experiment varies x only and falsifies Hx. A parent clue also includes an untested x-y coupling.

## Q15 — Simulator
A versioned external simulator generates observations under declared assumptions. Its output is proposed as if it were automatically a universal domain theorem.

## Q16 — Exhausted rationale
The last three experiments produced no new residual, discrimination, refinement, or model change. Hundreds of parameter combinations remain but no represented clue distinguishes them.

## Q17 — Cost choice
Two proposed experiments are both lawful and equally discriminating. A supplied profile ranks wall-clock cost. A third cheaper proposal would violate a required control.

## Q18 — Return to family
An experiment discovers a candidate invariant, refines one QU constraint, and reveals a transition-order difference. The investigator proposes EI directly mark the invariant true and the two carriers naturally identical.
