## Discovery discipline

Apply the current Discovery Protocols:

- observation precedes judgment;
- align semantic quantity, scope, layer, comparison view, authority and closure before collapsing or rejecting a candidate;
- discovery priority is not proof authority;
- a repeated surface form is a lead, not a theorem;
- common-core structure may coexist with load-bearing residuals;
- structural correspondence does not establish natural identity;
- unresolved structure must remain represented when load-bearing;
- qualification disposition and discovery disposition are independent;
- attractive commonality may be falsified;
- investigation may stop when the requested disposition has sufficient evidence.

## Experiment-specific anti-circularity

The native corpus deliberately assigns a different relation label to each module. No shared cross-module barrier relation is declared.

Therefore:

```text
same binary edge shape
    != proof of one semantic generator
```

A useful discovery must be supported by the lexical/provenance evidence plus structural comparison, and must state residual distinctions or falsifiers.

No source specification may be refactored as part of this experiment. A candidate common factor remains a discovery result until separately qualified.
