# Experiment 018 — DP 0.6 Cumulative Observation-First Holdout

**Purpose:** qualify DP 0.1–0.6 cumulative discovery behavior, with emphasis on DP 0.5 discrepancy handling and DP 0.6 current authority routing.

## D01 — Different counts reveal a hidden distinction

The same frozen evidence corpus is reported as 14 citation occurrences and 5 artifacts. Both definitions and values are correct.

Classify whether this is a direct value conflict and whether there is a structural discovery lead.

## D02 — Decoder error and discovery lead coexist

A decoder drops one explicit relation. Exact source comparison proves the decoder defect. Across several independent examples, the dropped relation is always derivable from two retained relations.

Determine the qualification disposition, the discovery disposition, and whether repair closes the derivability lead automatically.

## D03 — Explicit field versus derived semantic property

A stored boolean says `scope_metadata_present=false`. Exact surrounding structure nevertheless constrains the proposition to domain S.

Determine whether these observations are a direct boolean contradiction and whether the relation between explicit metadata and derived scope remains a discovery question.

## D04 — Coarse ontology splits

Several reports use the single label `source`, but preserved evidence distinguishes citation occurrence, artifact, event, lineage, and independence group.

Determine the appropriate discovery action.

## D05 — Safe scoped quotient

Two represented structures differ in internal factorization but exact evidence proves that the difference is irrelevant to frozen downstream observation set C.

Determine whether a scoped quotient/equivalence may be established and whether DP may call the subjects globally identical.

## D06 — Unresolved load-bearing factor

Two structures appear identical in all observed fields, but qualified QU contains an unresolved relation that could break the proposed correspondence.

Determine whether DP may collapse the structures and what discovery disposition remains appropriate.

## D07 — Dependency cone propagation

A newly established distinction falsifies a candidate quotient used by seven dependent hypotheses. Provenance is complete.

Determine whether DP should suppress propagation to keep the model stable or expose/reassess the dependency cone.

## D08 — Qualification can finish while discovery remains open

A representation has been shown complete and accurate under its declared contract. One preserved discrepancy still supports a plausible structural question that does not undermine that qualification.

Determine whether the representation may qualify and what happens to the discovery lead.

## D09 — Seductive anomaly falsified

A mismatch initially suggests a hidden equivalence. Quantity/scope alignment plus targeted falsification establishes an ordinary implementation error and no structural explanation survives.

Determine the final discovery disposition and whether an isomorphism should be manufactured.

## D10 — Bounded search

A local discrepancy is semantically aligned; one plausible hidden-structure explanation is tested and falsified; a straightforward defect owner is established.

Determine whether DP requires exhaustive search of every imaginable explanation before repair.

## D11 — Identity-dependent discovery routes through NEI 0.4

DP finds a deep structural correspondence between A and B. The intended downstream claim asks whether A and B are the same natural/domain object. No exact identity theorem is present in the DP evidence alone.

Determine whether DP may directly conclude SAME and which authority owns the identity conclusion.

## D12 — Non-identity discovery does not require NEI

DP compares two parser structures only to establish a scoped structural common core and explicit residual. No natural/domain identity conclusion is requested.

Determine whether NEI must be invoked merely because two subjects are being compared.

## D13 — QU is required when unresolved structure is load-bearing

A discrepancy depends on whether unresolved relation U exists. The QU 0.1 state represents U's admissible presence/absence family and constraints.

Determine whether DP should preserve that QU structure or replace it with a generic UNKNOWN token.

## D14 — Determinate case does not require artificial QU

A fully determined structural mismatch is established under adequate closure authority. No unresolved structure affects the claim.

Determine whether DP must manufacture a QU state solely because QU is a qualified dependency available to the family.
