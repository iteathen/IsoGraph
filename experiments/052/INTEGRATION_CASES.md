# Experiment 052 — Fresh full-stack integration cases

## I01 — Expected value versus reducible named relation
A frozen source defines RELAY(x,y) exactly through two lower incidences. A rendering keeps RELAY as one named node and an expected-output file disagrees with one reconstructed result. The lower definition is available, but not represented. No source contract validates the expected-output file.

## I02 — QU-sensitive mapping
A mapping between two labels has two qualified QU realizations. Under one realization a discrepancy indicates a swapped index; under the other the compared quantities are distinct. No evidence selects either realization.

## I03 — Local defect plus scoped behavioral sameness
A serializer bug causes two state encodings to share bytes. The bug is repaired. After repair, exhaustive continuation testing still proves the two states accept exactly the same future suffix language. Their provenance and internal structure remain distinct and no natural-identity theorem is supplied.

## I04 — Transition anatomy defect
A transition record contains source incidence and event incidence but omits destination incidence. The qualified DTS contract requires source, event, and destination anatomy. A validator accepted the malformed record.

## I05 — Useful derived frontier
A derived frontier summary is reconstructed exactly from primitive order/membership relations and greatly improves discovery. Removing the summary leaves every exact assertion reconstructable from the primitive kernel.

## I06 — Conflicting faithful authorities
Two external standards are faithfully represented and both claim applicability to revision R while requiring incompatible thresholds. No authority says recency, prestige, or publication order resolves the conflict.

## I07 — Arithmetic discovery view
A discovery pass notices that a derived COUNT_DIFFERENCE label is useful for finding a pattern. The source claim ultimately depends on exact list cardinalities and arithmetic comparison, but the authoritative graph contains only COUNT_DIFFERENCE and raw literals.

## I08 — Historical Core 0.19 artifact
A rendering was correctly qualified under Core 0.19 by terminating at a pinned qualified semantic construction. Core 0.20 later requires that construction to be expanded because its internal semantics are load-bearing. The historical artifact itself has not changed.

## I09 — Parse defect and surviving clue
A malformed delimiter drops one row. Before repair a recurrence motif is observed. After minimum syntax repair, the restored row also participates in the same motif, which is independently derivable from primitive incidences.

## I10 — Sidecar-only meaning
A native graph contains raw object G and named predicate GROUP(G). A prose sidecar defines group closure, identity, inverse, and associativity. None of those semantics occur in native support or a pinned qualified primitive expansion.

## I11 — Raw domain-flavored carriers
Raw carriers are named state_A and state_B. Their names imply nothing operational. Every transition, initial/final incidence, and load-bearing rule is represented separately in primitive structure. A reviewer objects that the word "state" is too domain-specific to be primitive.

## I12 — Green-test patch under uncertainty
A test expects 42 while implementation returns 43. A load-bearing mapping is still QU-bearing with two admissible realizations, one producing 42 and one 43. No authority selects a realization. A patch changes a constant only to make the test green.

## I13 — Derived-view deletion firewall after repair
A local representation defect is repaired. Exact assertion A has both a derived-view support path and a direct primitive support path. Deleting every derived-view node leaves A reconstructable from the repaired primitive relations.

## I14 — Reference challenge with direct evidence
A trusted reference and two independent derivations agree on -5. IsoGraph renders +5. Trace inspection finds a unary-minus parse omission in the rendering path.

## I15 — Scoped equality versus natural identity
Two objects have equal effective susceptibility on every non-target site and therefore identical phase transformers in the declared treatment scope. They differ on retained target-site metadata and remain separate source objects.

## I16 — Unknown lower definition and tempting abstraction
A load-bearing relation R is undefined in source and has no extensional closure declaration. DP finds a useful pattern if R is assumed transitive. No qualified authority establishes transitivity.
